Job Description

BACKGROUND

UNFPA is the lead United Nations agency for delivering a world where every pregnancy is intended, every childbirth is safe, and every young person's potential is fulfilled. The UNFPA Strategic Plan for 2026-2029 articulates the organization's response to a complex global environment, providing a roadmap for resilience and renewal. It is designed to accelerate the implementation of the Programme of Action of the International Conference on Population and Development (ICPD) and the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals by 2030. This mandate is pursued through a focus on four interconnected outcomes: ending the unmet need for family planning; ending preventable maternal deaths; ending gender-based violence and harmful practices; and adapting to demographic change through evidence and rights-based policies.

In Uzbekistan, UNFPA develops evidence-based advocacy messages, publications, and technical reports to mobilize decision-makers and the public in support of reproductive rights and gender equality across the country. The production of compelling, creative digital content is central to achieving our priority advocacy objectives. By leveraging multiple platforms and languages, our content highlights the diverse impact of UNFPA's work—from improving maternal health to empowering young people—thereby fostering public engagement and action while ensuring the highest standards of transparency and accountability.

To ensure timely, consistent, and high-quality delivery of communication and advocacy products, UNFPA Uzbekistan seeks to establish a Long-Term Arrangement (LTA) with qualified professional graphic designers and motion graphics specialists.

2. PURPOSE OF THE ASSIGNMENT

To meet these requirements, UNFPA seeks to engage highly creative and professional graphic designers and motion graphics editors to develop high-quality visual and multimedia materials that support advocacy, communication, and visibility of UNFPA programmes, contribute to awareness-raising on sexual and reproductive health and rights, and effectively engage the public, donors, partners, and other stakeholders across digital and print platforms.

3. PROGRAMME AREA AND SPECIFIC PROJECT AREA

The consultants will provide professional graphic design and motion graphics services, in line with UNFPA global branding and communication standards, to support advocacy, visibility, and reporting related to the implementation of the UNFPA Country Programme Document (CPD) 2026-2030 in Uzbekistan, across all programme areas, through digital, print, and multimedia platforms.

4. OBJECTIVE(S)

The objective is to establish a Long-Term Agreement (LTA) to enable timely, flexible, and efficient engagement of professional graphic designers and motion graphics editors for UNFPA Uzbekistan projects. The consultants will develop high-quality advocacy, communication, and emergency-related visual materials that demonstrate UNFPA's impact—particularly in maternal health, sexual and reproductive health and rights, and gender equality—in full compliance with UNFPA branding and communication standards, for use across digital, print, and multimedia platforms.

5. MAJOR TASKS TO BE ACCOMPLISHED

The consultants will provide high-quality graphic design and motion graphics services aligned with UNFPA's advocacy priorities and global branding standards. The content will support advocacy, visibility, and communication objectives across digital, print, and multimedia platforms, contributing to awareness-raising, audience engagement, and resource mobilization for UNFPA's mandate.

Tasks include, but are not limited to: - Develop professional graphic design and motion graphics products in line with UNFPA branding and communication guidelines.

- Deliver draft and final approved design products in ready-to-print and ready-to-upload formats, submitted via shared drive or other agreed digital channels (e.g. shared drive).

- Provide PDF versions of finalised products for archiving and publication on UNFPA platforms.

- Prepare printing and production specifications, in consultation with printers or production agencies when required.

- Create and deliver motion graphics and animated visual assets for digital and multimedia use.

- Collaborate with UNFPA communication and programme teams on visual concepts, layouts, colours, typography, animation, audio, and video elements.

- Apply contemporary graphic design techniques and trends relevant to advocacy and humanitarian communication.

- Review, edit, and consolidate materials to ensure clarity, consistency, and coherence of structure and key messages.

- Verify spelling, terminology, acronyms, and place names in accordance with official UNFPA terminology, Brand Toolkit, and style guidelines.

- Ensure compliance with UNFPA workflows and production toolkits.

- Participate in multiple rounds of revisions, as required.

-Incorporate feedback and implement revisions requested by UNFPA management and relevant partners until final validation and approval of deliverables.

- Deliver high-quality outputs within agreed timelines, including short-notice assignments when necessary.

- Adapt materials for different platforms and formats, including websites and social media platforms such as YouTube, Facebook, X/Twitter, and Instagram.

6. RESPONSIBILITY OF CONSULTANT:

The consultant will use their own equipment, including a laptop, software, and any other tools or accessories required to perform the assignment.
  • The consultant shall maintain strict confidentiality of all information, documents, briefs, materials, drafts, and unpublished content received in the course of the assignment and shall not disclose, share, or distribute such information to any third party without prior written authorization from UNFPA.
  • All intellectual property rights, including copyrights and related rights, arising from the performance of this assignment shall vest exclusively in UNFPA.
  • The consultant shall ensure that all materials used in the deliverables, including images, fonts, audio, video, and other assets, are original or properly licensed for use by UNFPA and do not infringe on third-party intellectual property rights.
  • The consultant shall not share, publish, or distribute any content or materials produced under this assignment without prior written permission from UNFPA.
  • The consultant shall not include their name, logo, signature, or emblem on any final products. Only UNFPA branding may be used, if required.
  • The consultant shall be available for coordination and regular communication with UNFPA focal points throughout the duration of each assignment.
  • The consultant may be required to work outside standard working hours, including Saturdays and Sundays, depending on assignment needs. Such work shall be considered part of the agreed consultancy fee, unless otherwise specified in writing.

RESPONSIBILITIES OF UNFPA:

UNFPA will:

Provide clear and timely briefs, background information, and technical guidance for each assignment.
  • Share relevant brand guidelines, templates, logos, reference materials, and communication standards, as applicable.
  • Facilitate coordination with programme and communication colleagues, as required.
  • Review submitted deliverables and provide consolidated feedback in a timely manner.
  • Approve final deliverables in accordance with UNFPA internal procedures.

- Ability to collaborate effectively with creative teams, communication specialists, and programme stakeholders.

-Strong organizational and time-management skills; ability to manage multiple assignments simultaneously. - - Excellent communication skills, with the ability to receive, interpret, and apply feedback constructively.
