At the World Bank, a member of the World Bank Group (WBG), you’ll join a diverse, global community working across cultures, disciplines, and borders to address the world’s most pressing development challenges. With 1.2 billion young people reaching working age in the decade ahead, the challenge of job creation has never been greater. Through partnerships across 145 countries and more than 182 offices worldwide, we work with governments, private sector, development partners and other stakeholders to invest in people, strengthen markets, and deliver scalable, data-driven solutions that generate more and better jobs and improve lives. For more information, visit https://www.worldbank.org/en/who-we-are/ibrd

The World Bank Group (WBG) Infrastructure Vertical provides public and private solutions to operational teams and clients across the WBG to produce scalable impact. Its mandate is to deliver knowledge for impact to support the WBG to achieve its goals in support of our mission and specifically: providing guidance on creating more and better jobs by: (i) supporting foundational infrastructure and human capital, (ii) policy environment, and (iii) enabling and mobilizing private sector capital. With 60 years of experience, the WBG is poised to support the sector becoming an engine of growth and jobs through improving policies and governance, foundational infrastructure and private sector investments. WBG lending in the sector is expected to grow substantially. The Infrastructure VPU’s objectives in driving outcomes include replicating and scaling effective solutions, enhancing thought leadership and innovation, and delivering timely knowledge to client teams. For more information: https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/infrastructure

The Disaster Management, Solutions & Impact Unit (KIUSD) supports low and middle-income countries to understand, manage, and reduce their risks from natural hazards and climate change. Within KIUSD, the Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR) umbrella trust fund is a global partnership that helps low- and middle-income countries better understand and reduce their vulnerability to natural hazards and climate change. GFDRR supports analytics and technical assistance to governments in the most vulnerable countries and communities where it will have the greatest positive impact. As a donor-funded program, GFDRR consults regularly with donors and actively strives to maintain and develop new partnerships for increased impact. For more information: https://www.gfdrr.org/en

GFDRR follows a demand-driven model, linking country needs with technical expertise, financing, and partnerships to deliver impact at scale. GFDRR’s 2026-20230 strategy emphasizes measurable results, introducing a strengthened results framework with specific quantitative targets, including a goal to improve climate resilience for 100 million people by 2030.

Each thematic area invests in knowledge and tools development being responsive to the needs of WBG clients. Tools range from policy and regulatory frameworks review, stakeholder engagements, geospatial analysis combined with solution suitability analysis, rapid post-disaster assessments, risk quantification (some tool and their applications can be accessed on GFDRR’s website). Additionally, GFDRR has teams dedicated to grant monitoring and GFDRR impact evaluation and learning, as well as communications and knowledge management.

Please note this position is financed by a Trust Fund and renewal is contingent upon availability of funding, in addition to other factors, including but not limited to business needs, staff performance and skills fit.

Job Duties and Accountabilities:

Role Purpose:
GFDRR is seeking a Software Engineer to support AI-upskilling of GFDRR staff, develop AI solutions, and provide strategic advice for the overall unit’s AI-enablement. The Software Engineer will drive GFDRR’s technology learning and adoption process, capability roadmaps, and learning ecosystems that enable GFDRR’s thematic areas to build critical skills in AI and associated solutions such as cloud use, data governance and platform engineering. To this end the Software Engineer will seek to understand the different workflows and GFDRR and advice on AI/ML architecture integration. The Consultant will be responsible for enabling learning at scale—embedding capability development within thematic areas and program management teams, leveraging internal expertise, and integrating external learning solutions. Operating through influence, and collaborative, strategic partnerships within GFDRR, WBG and external parties.

In close collaboration with GFDRR DRM lead and Senior M&E Specialists, the Software Engineer is responsible for promoting AI-integration while enhancing GFDRR’s capacity to scale up and deliver solutions that can effectively drive impact. The Software Engineer is expected to collaborate with all thematic areas and GFDRR program management teams.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Role- and Solution-Based Skill Development: Design learning pathways for hands-on AI-solutions upskilling/reskilling of GFDRR staff, from basic digital fluency to advanced technical expertise. Advise product, technology, and enablement teams as well as leaders and staff on learning paths.
  • Learning Roadmaps: Develop and manage technology learning strategies integrating WBG training solutions and identifying external, complementary solutions. Create integrated learning roadmaps that translate technology investments into clear skill-building pathways and efficiency gains.
  • Solution Development: Lead the integration of AI-solutions in prioritized workflows, such as GFDRR’s M&E function. The Senior Consultant will work closely with the M&E team in the development of WBG and GFDRR grant portfolio review framework and tool, including reviewing existing framework, developing the analytical platform, testing accuracy and reporting at each development stage and completion. The consultant will be responsible for coordinating with WB ITS team for the identification and authorization to use available APIs and data platforms. The tool should be well annotated, utilize version control mechanisms, comply with wider code repositories and overall tests and documentation. Solutions will be self-documenting when applicable and accompanied by clear documentation and manuals for easy future use. Solution development must embed automated provenance tracking like cryptographic watermarking to verify content authenticity, enforce strict compliance mapping against World Bank data classification guidelines, and implement multi-layered RAG guardrails that dynamically redact sensitive personal data during retrieval, citation, and output generation.
  • Workflow Automation & Agentic AI: Engineer and prototype robust, transparent AI agents for complex multi-step workflows. Implement hardened execution guardrails—including sandboxing, least-privilege permission scoping, and Human-in-command (HIC) and/or Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) checkpoints—to design and govern agentic reasoning loops and deliver secure, auditable, and reliable autonomy and drive safe efficiency.
  • Advisory & Stakeholder Leadership: Serve as a trusted advisor to GFDRR on technology adoption, particularly AI platforms, including aspects of data governance, and cloud and data management solutions.
  • Coordination: Promote internal and external collaboration for AI-solutions integration and learning. Advise and take initiative to start strategic collaboration with WBG IT team and other WBG teams, or internally at GFDRR based on gaps identified for tool development.
  • Contribute to program deliverables, including preparation of GFDRR communication and dissemination materials, results monitoring, reporting, budgeting, and branding.
  • Influence capability development at enterprise scale, not just within a single tool or team.
  • Conduct other tasks, as needed, to support the overall objective of the position.

Selection Criteria

  • Advanced University Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Science, Statistics, or related field with strong computational elements, with minimum five years of relevant experience; a bachelor’s degree with a minimum of 7 years of relevant experience, or equivalent combination of education and experience.
  • At least five years of progressively responsible professional experience (internships excluded), with a focus on software development and integration of technologies.
  • At least three years of experience designing and delivering hands-on capacity building programs for enterprises.

Technical expertise:

  • Demonstrated expertise in designing and building autonomous, multi-step AI agents and utilizing advanced orchestration frameworks.
  • Expert-level knowledge of Large Language Models (LLMs), generative AI architectures, and text analytics, including Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques.
  • Extensive experience implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures using enterprise-grade managed search services and vector databases, incorporating continuous feedback loops (e.g., human-in-the-loop/human-in-command labeling, relevance tuning) to improve retrieval quality.
  • Demonstrated ability to design, develop, and deploy scalable, high-quality software and AI solutions across multiple platforms. Hands-on experience building enterprise applications that meet complex business requirements with minimal supervision.
  • Strong programming, geospatial, and quantitative skills, including fluency in Python for geospatial workflows, cloud computing, Google Earth Engine, and open-source GIS tools (e.g., QGIS).
  • Experience with and/or interest in disaster risk management, urban planning, civil infrastructure systems and financing are strongly preferred.

International & Interpersonal Skills:

  • International experience and demonstrated ability to work in diverse, multicultural environments are required.
  • Knowledge of the World Bank key Resilience and Disaster Risk Management priorities and programs is a plus but not essential.
  • Excellent organizational skills with ability to work under tight deadlines and handle multiple requests simultaneously, while being detail oriented.
  • Mentors and guides junior engineers to elevate the team’s core competencies, while simultaneously establishing foundational AI practices, such as enterprise prompt design frameworks and user personalization strategies.
  • Excellent written and spoken English is essential; knowledge of other languages—especially Spanish, French, and Portuguese—is highly desirable.
