The International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD) is an international financial institution and a specialized United Nations agency dedicated to eradicating rural poverty and hunger. It does so by investing in rural people. IFAD finances programmes and projects that increase agricultural productivity and raise rural incomes, and advocates at the local, national and international level for policies that contribute to rural transformation.

The Office of Technical Delivery is responsible for providing expert technical guidance to IFAD teams engaged in the design and implementation of projects and programmes to deliver the high technical quality investments that facilitate rural transformation, and which comply with IFAD's mandate and operational policies. The Office ensures IFAD applies and disseminates state of the art knowledge in its investments and evidence on strategic themes and encourages the innovative application of global knowledge and evidence in IFAD investments. This helps countries tackle complex development challenges in fostering inclusive and sustainable rural transformation including mainstreaming areas. The Office of Technical Delivery (OTD) is led by a Managing Director and is comprised of two (2) divisions: the Environment, Climate and Peoples Division (ECP) and the Production, Markets and Infrastructure Division (PMI).

The Production, Markets and Infrastructure Division (PMI) facilitates improved and efficient delivery of IFAD's corporate mandate, predominantly through the quality of the design and implementation of its policies, country strategies and projects. Key functions and activities of PMI include Quality Enhancement, Policy Engagement, Networking and Strategic Partnerships. PMI's technical mandate covers a range of themes, all of which are key to deliver IFAD's strategic framework, including: agronomy, livestock, fisheries and aquaculture, markets and value chains, agriculture research for development (AR4D), ICT4D, inclusive rural finance (including PARM, INSURED and remittances), rural institutions and farmers organizations, rural infrastructure, water, land and natural resources management.

PMI is led by a Divisional Director and is comprised of four (4) units: Production & Nutrition Unit; Land & Water Unit; Value Chains & Technology Unit; and Rural Finance Unit.

The Agribusiness Linkages Facility (Agri-Link) creates direct partnerships between IFAD's donor partners, smallholder farmers and the private sector. Together with local and international agribusinesses, it implements targeted, time-bound interventions that strengthen these companies- supply chains by improving farmers- ability to meet market requirements. The facility supports private investment in rural areas and contributes to higher farmer income, improved food security and rural employment.

Job Role

The incumbent is a professional in a specialized thematic area, reporting either to a more senior Technical Specialist or to the divisional Director. At this level Technical Specialists analyse technical and policy problems of concern to IFAD, intergovernmental bodies, and national authorities and provide technical input to the development of policies, technical strategies and proposals and project implementation. The incumbent may work collaboratively with the thematic Technical Specialists, Country Directors and other staff to ensure consistency, cohesion and synergy in the application of technical advice and provision of programme development support.

Position specifics: The incumbent will strengthen PMI's capacity to support IFAD's Markets and Value Chains (MVC) and private sector engagement work. The Technical Specialist will provide technical expertise and operational support to country-level and global operations, with a focus on the Agribusiness Linkages Facility (Agri-Link), where the incumbent acts as focal point for, and supports coordination of Agri-link activities, under the guidance of the Lead Global Technical Specialist, Markets and Value Chains PMI.

The incumbent supports the development and implementation of Agri-Link, an initiative connecting IFAD donor partners, smallholder producers and the private sector. The role generates operational evidence and results to inform institutional decision-making and strengthen IFAD's private sector engagement approach within its sovereign portfolio. The role is embedded within PMI (OTD) and contributes directly to IFAD corporate workstreams on private sector engagement in sovereign operations and may lead or coordinate related initiatives.
