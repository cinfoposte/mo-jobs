Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

UN Women works with the Central and the State Governments in India to strengthen Gender Responsive Budgeting (GRB) systems to institutionalize gender responsive public finance management. It supports Union ministries, state Finance, Planning and Women and Child Development Departments, Panchayati Raj institutions and national and state training institutions. The work involves providing technical support in state and district level budget analysis, Gender Budget Statement reporting, gender responsive Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDP), capacity building of officials and elected women representatives, and research on emerging areas of public finance.

As part of this technical support to Government, UNW India it has supported growing body of research studies, analytical products and proposals. These include departmental and sectoral gender budget analyses, district level analyses of GPDP and scheme expenditure, a research study on GRB, fiscal devolution and the Sixteenth Finance Commission, a scoping study on responsive budgeting for a green and circular economy and just transition, policy briefs, guidance notes, training modules and concept notes for future programme.

To ensure that these products are technically sound, policy relevant and fit for use by government, UN Women seeks to engage three Consultants / Senior Advisors, , to undertake an independent technical review of these outputs and to advise on future research. Each Advisor will be a lead in their own discipline. The three disciplines are:

  • Public Policy and Governance: gender mainstreaming, policy formulation, institutional mechanisms and public administration.
  • Economics and Public Finance: fiscal policy, gender budget analysis, public expenditure, resource allocation and distributional impacts.
  • Infrastructure, Energy and Sustainable Development: gender responsive infrastructure, energy transition including circular economy, transport, water and sanitation, climate finance, and equitable access to public services.

Gender analysis is integral to all three disciplines. Each Advisor is expected to apply GRB and gender analysis within their own field, while the GRB Unit of UNW India Country Office will provide the programme and gender equality perspective.

The Terms of Reference in this individual assignment covers the position of Senior Advisor, Economics and Public Finance, GRB being at the core through public finance perspective. The Consultant/Advisor under this assignment will provide analysis on sound fiscal data, correct budget classification and rigorous economic reasoning to set the direction of future economic and fiscal research on GRB.

II. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Objective

To provide independent technical review and strategic advice to the UN Women India on the economic and fiscal quality of its research studies, analytical products and proposals, and to recommend ways to strengthen their analytical rigour, policy relevance and use by government.

Scope of Work

Under the overall supervision of the Coordinator- Gender Responsive Budgeting , UN Women India Country Office, the Senior Advisor will:

Review assigned GRB products from an economics and public finance perspective, including but not limited to:

  • Analysis of Union and state Gender Budget Statements
  • Departmental and sectoral gender budget analyses
  • District and Gram Panchayat level analyses of GPDP, MGNREGS and scheme expenditure
  • The research study on GRB, fiscal devolution and the Sixteenth Finance Commission
  • Policy briefs, guidance notes and knowledge products on public finance
  • Concept notes and proposals for future research.

Assess the quality of each product with respect to:

  • Accuracy of fiscal data and correct use of budget documents, including Budget Estimates, Revised Estimates and Actuals
  • Soundness of methods used to estimate gender allocations, including the Part A, Part B and Part C classification of Gender Budget Statements
  • Analysis of allocation, release and expenditure, and the link between outlays and outcomes
  • Treatment of fiscal federalism, intergovernmental transfers, Union and State Finance Commission grants and local body finances
  • Distributional and incidence analysis of public spending and revenue measures
  • Robustness of economic reasoning and of the policy implications drawn
  • Practical use for Finance and Planning departments.

Identify analytical gaps, data limitations, methodological weaknesses and areas for further research in fiscal and economic analysis.

Advise on analytical tools and methods such as costing of gender commitments, expenditure tracking, benefit incidence analysis, gender budget tagging and fiscal space assessment.

Advise on proposals for future research in public finance, including options for gender integration in the work of the Seventeenth Finance Commission, state budgets and local body finances.

Provide written comments in tracked changes and a short review note for each assigned product.

Take part in meetings of the Committee convened by the Chair, and present findings and recommendations.

At the request of UN Women, provide technical inputs for engagement with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Women and Child Development, state Finance and Planning departments and Finance Commission processes, and join coordination meetings where UN Women is present.

Identify potential partnership and resource mobilization opportunities in this area for UN Women's consideration.

Prepare a consolidated technical review report with strategic recommendations for future work in this discipline.

GRB Research Review and Advisory Committee

UN Women India will constitute a GRB Research Review and Advisory Committee (the Committee) as an internal advisory and monitoring mechanism. Its purpose is to guide the GRB Unit on completed research studies, ongoing analyses and proposals for future work. The arrangements are as follows:

  • The Committee will be chaired by the Coordinator- Gender Responsive Budgeting, UN Women India Country Office, who will convene its meetings, set the agenda and approve its minutes and recommendations.
  • The GRB Unit will serve as the Secretariat. It will circulate review material at least seven working days before each meeting, record the proceedings and track follow up action.
  • The three Senior Advisors, engaged under separate Terms of Reference, will take part as members in an individual advisory capacity. The Chair may invite other UN Women colleagues and external experts as required.
  • The Chair will convene at least one in-person meeting at UN House, New Delhi and at least two online meetings during the contract period, each of up to two hours.
  • The recommendations of the Committee are advisory. UN Women will decide on their adoption and use based on recommendation and approval from the Senior Management Team.
  • The Advisor is individually responsible for the deliverables in this Terms of Reference, which will be assessed and paid on an individual basis. Participation in the Committee does not create any joint or collective deliverable.
  • The Advisor will not represent UN Women or speak on its behalf. Engagement with Union and state government officials will take place only at the request of UN Women and in its presence.
  • Travel for any state level engagement request will be supported separately as per UN Women travel policy, rules and procedures.

Deliverables:

  • Inception note and review plan agreed with the Programme Lead, GRB, listing the assigned products, the economic and fiscal review criteria and a work schedule - Within two weeks of contract signing - 10% payment
  • First technical review: tracked comments and a short review note on each product in the first batch - By the end of Month 2 - 25% payment
  • Advisory note on strengthening fiscal analysis in GRB products, covering budget classification, costing, expenditure tracking, Finance Commission transfers and local body finances, presented at a Committee meeting - By the end of Month 3 - 20% payment
  • Second technical review: tracked comments and review notes on the second batch of products, with written advice on proposals for future research in public finance - By the end of Month 4 - 25% payment
  • Consolidated Technical Review Report setting out strengths, gaps, recommendations and a forward agenda for economic and fiscal research on GRB - By the end of Month 5 - 10% payment
  • Finalization of the Report - By the end of Month 6 - 10% payment

Expected Outputs:

  • Technical review of the assigned products from an economics and public finance perspective.
  • Written comments and a review note for each product.
  • Identification of gaps in fiscal data, methods and economic analysis.
  • An advisory note on fiscal analysis methods for GRB.
  • A consolidated report with a forward agenda for economic and fiscal research on GRB.
