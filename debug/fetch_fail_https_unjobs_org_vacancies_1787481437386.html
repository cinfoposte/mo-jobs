What will you do in this position?

The Ministry of Social Development (MoSD) is implementing its Sector Strategy 2025-2027 alongside the Shock-Responsive Social Protection (SRSP) Protocol, with the objective of strengthening the effectiveness, sustainability, and responsiveness of the social protection system in the West Bank and Gaza. Achieving these objectives requires a more systematic approach to mobilizing financial resources that are aligned with national priorities and institutional capacities.

Within the framework of the Joint SDG Fund programme, WFP is supporting MoSD along other partners with an output on strengthening sustainable financing and institutional arrangements for the social protection sector. As part of this output, there is a need to support MoSD in clarifying how fundraising for the sector can be approached in a more structured, evidence-based, and proactive manner, moving beyond ad-hoc or opportunity-driven efforts.

Recent consultations highlighted the need for visibility over the full financing landscape and a consolidated plan that links institutional priorities with potential funding sources. The purpose of this assignment is to support MoSD in developing a clear, practical, and realistic fundraising plan that strengthens sustainable financing for the social protection sector, in line with the MoSD Sector Strategy 2025-2027 and the SRSP Protocol. The fundraising plan will serve as the main planning and reference tool to help MoSD articulate its funding needs, identify appropriate sources of support, and approach resource mobilization in a more structured and forward-looking manner.

Why work with WFP Palestine?

For decades, Palestine has been facing a protracted and complex protection and humanitarian crisis. The humanitarian situation worsened alarmingly after an upsurge in violence from October 2023.

WFP, through our dual mandate in humanitarian and development, provides life-saving food assistance to the most vulnerable and food insecure non-refugee Palestinians.

WFP provides logistics support to humanitarian and development partners, allowing them to reach more people faster, and at a lower cost to donors and the environment. By offering our cash-based transfer platform to the wider humanitarian and development community, coordinating logistics, and developing an inter-agency common feedback mechanism, WFP contributes to wider humanitarian efforts.

How can you make a difference?

Under the supervision of WFP, and in close collaboration with the Ministry of Social Development (MoSD), the consultant will:

  • Review and analyze the current financing and fundraising landscape for the social protection sector, including existing grants, funding agreements, and financing arrangements, and assess who MoSD is currently approaching for funding and which thematic areas are being financed.
  • Assess the scale, duration, and predictability of existing funding, its alignment with the MoSD Strategy and SRSP Protocol, and identify recurring funding gaps, underfunded areas, and internal institutional/capacity constraints affecting resource mobilization.
  • Identify and map potential funding sources and fundraising mechanisms that MoSD could realistically pursue, including bilateral and multilateral partners, IFIs, UN agencies, foundations, CSR and private sector actors, diaspora, and other non-traditional/innovative financing channels, with actionable steps on how MoSD could tap into each.
  • Conduct a targeted data-gathering exercise, in coordination with MoSD, to identify priority programmes, planned interventions, and operational needs across MoSD departments and units, and translate these into a structured set of funding needs.
  • Develop MoSD's prioritized funding plan, linking social protection priorities and programmes to funding needs and fundable investment packages, and proposing practical approaches for engaging potential funders/donors, including a phased and realistic roadmap.
  • Develop a comprehensive Fundraising Framework for the social protection sector, covering fundraising approaches and methods, donor segmentation and engagement strategies, and communication, visibility, and advocacy channels to support fundraising.
  • Outline key donor requirements and expectations, and propose a realistic institutional, human resource, and budget plan for fundraising, including recommended structures, roles, and systems for tracking, reporting, and donor coordination.
  • Organize and facilitate a consultative validation workshop to review findings and validate strategic recommendations and organize and facilitate a Ministry-led Fundraising Event (funded by WFP) to present the final framework and mobilize donor interest.
  • Support MoSD in developing and submitting at least one to two fund-ready proposals aligned with identified funding opportunities.
  • Prepare an Inception Report and a Final Report, with deliverables submitted in English, and the fundraising plan submitted in both English and Arabic, with key summaries translated into Arabic upon request.
