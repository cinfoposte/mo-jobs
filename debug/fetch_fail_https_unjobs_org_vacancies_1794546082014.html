Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

Through its programmes and projects, UN Women is providing technical assistance to national partners (governmental and non-governmental), including private sector, in the implementation of existing international and national commitments to women’s rights and gender equality, it facilitates networking and exchange of good practices and advocates for women’s rights and gender equality in all areas of life.

The work of UN Women in the Republic of Moldova is guided by its Country Strategic Note 2023 - 2027 , aligned to the UN-Moldova Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF) for 2023-2027 and the UN Women Global Strategic Plan (SP) (2022-2025), and aims to contribute to the gender-responsive implementation of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.

The overarching vision of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development is inclusive and sustainable growth with its promise to leave no one behind. Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 5, target 5.5. calls to “ensure women’s full and effective participation and equal opportunities for leadership at all levels of the decision-making in political, economic and public life”. Gender equality is mainstreamed throughout the SDGs, including SDG 8 “Decent Work and Economic Growth”. Gender equality by 2030 requires urgent action to eliminate the many root causes of discrimination that still curtail women’s rights in private and public spheres. At its core, the economic empowerment of women depends on the quantity and quality of paid employment, the provision or absence of public services, the amount of unpaid care work borne by women, as well as coverage or lack thereof under core social and labor protections.

The Country Strategic Note 2023 - 2027 focuses on four impact areas: 1. Governance & participation in Public Life 2. Economic Empowerment & Resilience, 3 Ending Violence against women & girls, 4 Peace& Security, Humanitarian Action & Disaster Risk Reduction. To achieve progress under these areas, UN Women works with variety of national and international partners and as part of different national and regional initiatives.

For the economic empowerment and resilience of women, UN Women works to strengthen capacities of the Parliament and Government to develop, adopt and implement legal, regulatory and policy frameworks, as well as care service provision models that create decent work for women and that support the reduction and redistribution of unpaid care work and more equal sharing of care and domestic work between women and men, including developing the legal framework and piloting for alternative childcare services.

UN Women collaborates with various institutional actors, as well as with the Economic Council within the Prime Minister of the Republic of Moldova, in order to promote gender-sensitive programs for women employed and to implement the Roadmap on Women's Economic Empowerment.

UN Women will also seek to improve women’s access to male-dominated professions, notably in science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM) and build their information and communication technology (ICT) skills, and promote women’s increased access to economic opportunities through supporting best practices of the private sector.

The cooperation with the private sector covers the work on promotion The Women’s Empowerment Principles (WEPs) . Companies becomes more aware about the WEPs and knowledge products and tools to guide private sector institutions on WEPs implementation and monitoring in their workplaces and industries etc. were developed. Further work is required to continue on promotion of WEPs within the private sector.

UN Women also provides advisory and financial support to small producers to contribute to the development of their business, product exports and job creation in rural areas.

Key partners: Parliament and relevant Parliamentary Commissions, Ministry of Economy and Ministry of Labor and Social Protection, Economic Council under the Prime Minister, ODA National Agency of Employment, National, local and public institutions, Businesswomen associations and Employers Union, Private companies and Chamber of Commerce, Academia, rural CSOs and women-activists, humanitarian/refugee response actors, media; UN agencies – UNDP, UNFPA, ILO, UN Global Compact and the international community, i.e. USAID, World Bank, EBRD, EU, Trust Funds and other.

Therefore, UN Women is looking for a national consultant with expertise in the area of gender equality and women economic empowerment (WEE), to provide programmatic support to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of WEE portfolio of UN Women Country Office.

Description of Responsibilities /Scope of Work

The selected national consultant will be responsible to support timely and synergetic implementation of the UN Women Strategic Note in the area of women’s economic empowerment. The consultant will provide programmatic support in the development and implementation of effective programmes consistent with UN Women rules and regulations. She/he works in close collaboration with the operations, programme and projects’ staff in the UN Women[RU1] Moldova CO. The consultant shall report directly to the designated UN Women Moldova Programme Analyst with overall oversight by the UN Women WEPP Project Manager and the Women Economic Empowerment and Resilience Specialist.

The Consultant will undertake the following tasks:

Support the review of economic development policies, with focus on those related to entrepreneurship and export promotion, legal frameworks and programmes, to integrate gender equality and women’s economic empowerment priorities.

  • Provide strategic and technical support across the WEE portfolio, including programme planning and development, research and knowledge management, partnership building and coordination, policy dialogue, preparation of technical documents and knowledge products, and quality assurance of programme activities and deliverables.
  • Support the coordination of training delivery with partners and ensure follow-up on the application of acquired knowledge in institutional practice.

Support initiatives to improve women’s access to finance

  • Support access to finance work with financial institutions, including banks, microfinance institutions.
  • Support the development and implementation of institutional strategies, policies and action plans that advance gender equality and women’s financial inclusion.
  • Contribute to the design, implementation and assessment of financial and non-financial support approaches responsive to women’s needs, including those of women entrepreneurs.
  • Contribute to the development of practical guidance, tools and capacity-building initiatives to strengthen institutional responsiveness, women’s financial capabilities and readiness to access financing.
  • Support the use of research, sex-disaggregated data and monitoring tools to assess gaps, track progress and inform improvements in financial inclusion programmes and services.
  • Document implementation experience and contribute to the development of knowledge products on access to finance.

Support women’s entrepreneurship and business support programmes for women entrepreneurs

  • Support the development of sectoral gender and value chain analysis particularly in agriculture and food industries and ensure that findings inform programme design.
  • Contribute to the design and implementation of business support programmes and integrated business support approaches, that strengthen women’s entrepreneurship, business resilience, competitiveness and participation in domestic and international markets.
  • Support women entrepreneurs’ networks and mentors, rural cluster development and links to existing business infrastructure.
  • Support women’s access to vocational education and advocacy campaigns on care responsibilities and gender stereotypes.
  • Provide technical guidance to partner institutions and business support organisations to integrate gender-responsive approaches into their programmes, services and delivery mechanisms.
  • Contribute to the development of methodologies, practical tools and partnership arrangements for piloting and expanding business support initiatives.

Support a structured approach to company-level WEPs implementation and results monitoring

  • Guide companies in identifying and using relevant existing WEPs tools, guidance and resources, adapting their application to the specific needs, capacities and priorities of individual companies.
  • Support companies in assessing gaps in their existing policies and practices, using relevant WEPs assessment tools, employee consultations, internal policies and available organisational data.
  • Provide technical guidance to translate assessment findings and WEPs commitments into practical company-level actions, priorities and implementation plans.
  • Support companies in developing, reviewing or strengthening internal policies and practices related to gender equality, including equal pay, recruitment, career progression, women’s leadership, care responsibilities, flexible work arrangements, safe and respectful workplaces and gender-responsive procurement.
  • Help companies apply relevant WEPs tools to track progress and document changes in policies, workplace practices and organisational approaches, using existing WEPs indicators and reporting resources where applicable.
  • Maintain regular follow-up with WEPs signatories to identify implementation challenges, technical assistance needs and opportunities for further support from UN Women.
  • Facilitate peer exchange among WEPs signatories, enabling companies to share practical approaches, challenges, lessons learned and examples of WEPs implementation.
  • Document company experiences, practices and results to inform UN Women’s technical assistance, WEPs engagement, advocacy and recognition of good workplace practices.

Provide strategic advice, partner coordination, knowledge management and technical quality assurance across the WEE portfolio

  • Support the implementation of UN Women’s Strategic Note and WEE area action plans, providing substantive inputs to programme planning, development and implementation.
  • Gather, analyse and synthesise country-level information, research, policy papers, case studies and relevant international practices to inform programme priorities and identify opportunities for adaptation at national and local levels.
  • Contribute to the development of programme proposals, concepts, knowledge products, tools and other initiatives adapted to the national context, in collaboration with the UN Women team and implementing partners.
  • Develop and expand partnerships with public institutions, financial institutions, companies, business associations, civil society, academia, media and development partners, including non-traditional partners at national and local levels.
  • Support the conceptualisation and organisation of multistakeholder dialogues, seminars and conferences; contribute substantive expertise to partner and programme coordination meetings and support follow-up on agreed actions.
  • Document lessons learned and good practices, and prepare analytical briefs, case studies and technical content for outreach, communication, advocacy and donor visibility products, including fact sheets, infographics, campaigns and social media, in collaboration with relevant UN Women staff.
  • Undertake other tasks directly related to the WEE assignment and within its scope, as agreed with the supervisor or assigned by the Programme Manager.
  • Prepare concepts and terms of reference for relevant workplan components and review the technical quality of reports and deliverables produced by consultants, service providers and implementing partners.

Workplace and Official Travel

No travels are envisaged under the current assignment. In the case of unforeseeable travel, payment of travel costs including tickets, lodging and terminal expenses should be agreed upon, between UN Women and the National Consultant, prior to travel and will be reimbursed.

Financial Arrangements

Payment will be disbursed on a monthly basis, upon submission and approval of deliverables and timesheets with actual days worked and certification by the UN Women responsible staff that the services have been satisfactorily performed.

Performance Evaluation

Consultant’s performance will be evaluated against such criteria as: timeliness, responsibility, initiative, communication, accuracy, and quality of the products delivered.
