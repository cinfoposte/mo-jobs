Background and Context

The United Nations Entity for Gender Equality and the Empowerment of Women (UN Women) is the UN entity dedicated to gender equality and the empowerment of women. In Pakistan, UN Women and UNFPA co-chair the UN Gender Theme Group (GTG), the inter-agency coordination mechanism on Gender Equality and Women’s Empowerment (GEWE) under the UN Country Team (UNCT).

As part of ongoing efforts to strengthen gender equality accountability, the UN in Pakistan is developing a Country Gender Equality Profile (CGEP), a comprehensive, intersectional and evidence-based analysis of the state of GEWE in Pakistan. The CGEP is a formal deliverable under the GTG Workplan 2026 and responds to the global Gender Equality Acceleration Plan (GEAP). Its development will take into consideration the GEAP, the UNCT Gender Strategy for Pakistan 2025–2027, the UNCT-SWAP Gender Equality Scorecard and other relevant guidance and tools. It will serve as a primary evidence base for the Country Analysis (CA) and planning under the UNSDCF, including its extension to 2029.

The CGEP is a UNCT product, developed and owned collectively by all UN agencies, with the GTG co-chairs (UN Women and UNFPA) providing technical leadership in partnership with the Ministry of Human Rights (MoHR). This consultancy is funded by UN Women.

The CGEP Consultant will have overall responsibility for the research, evidence generation, stakeholder consultations, analysis, drafting, validation, fact-checking and finalization required for the development of the CGEP. This includes undertaking and/or coordinating the literature and desk review, collection and analysis of primary and secondary data, national and provincial consultations, key informant interviews (KIIs), focus group discussions (FGDs), translation of relevant consultation inputs, and preparation of analytical and drafting inputs for the final CGEP.

Scope of the CGEP:

The CGEP will cover the national level (Federal/ICT) and all four provinces (Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan) as well as AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan. Terminology and arrangements for stakeholder engagement in AJK and GB will be agreed during inception.

It will include provincial and national stakeholder consultations, comprehensive gender analysis aligned with the 12 critical areas of the Beijing Platform for Action (BPfA), The analysis and recommendations will take into consideration the global GEAP, the UNCT Gender Strategy for Pakistan 2025–2027, the UNSDCF, the UNCT-SWAP Gender Equality Scorecard and other relevant frameworks and tools.

The process will include a comprehensive literature and desk review, review and analysis of national and sub-national quantitative and qualitative data, stakeholder mapping, KIIs, FGDs and multi-stakeholder consultations at national and provincial levels, including engagement with government, civil society, women’s rights organizations, organisations of persons with disabilities (OPDs), academia, development partners, UN entities and other relevant stakeholders, as identified through the approved methodology.

The CGEP Consultant will actively engage with the UN Gender Theme Group (GTG), the Programme Management Team (PMT), the CGEP Task Team and other relevant inter-agency groups, as well as the Ministry of Human Rights, throughout the CGEP development process. This includes presenting the Inception Report and draft findings to relevant UN coordination structures, incorporating inter-agency feedback, and facilitating consultations with relevant stakeholders at national and provincial levels.

The primary objective of this consultancy is to deliver a comprehensive Country Gender Equality Profile (CGEP) for Pakistan, including the underlying research, evidence collection, stakeholder consultations, qualitative and quantitative analysis, drafting, support to validation, and finalization, in line with UN Women’s corporate CGEP guidance and the UNSDCF. The CGEP will provide evidence-based findings and recommendations to inform gender-transformative programming, GEWE financing discussions and UNCT gender equality accountability, taking into consideration the global GEAP and the UNCT Gender Strategy for Pakistan 2025–2027.

The assignment comprises 40 working days distributed across the contract period, including time for revisions. The Inception Report will establish an agreed review and clearance schedule, identifying submission dates, reviewer responsibilities and deadlines for consolidated feedback. The GTG Secretariat will coordinate feedback from the relevant national, inter-agency and regional reviewers. Final submission will incorporate agreed comments received within this schedule. Any changes to contractual deadlines or the level of effort will require approval through the applicable UN Women contract amendment procedures.

Guiding Principles:

The CGEP must be grounded in international norms and standards (2030 Agenda, CEDAW, BPfA, UNSCR 1325), apply an intersectional and LNOB lens, ensure national ownership through active engagement of government and non-governmental counterparts, and apply a do-no-harm approach throughout.

Description of Responsibilities/ Scope of Work

Within the assignment, the Consultant will have overall responsibility for undertaking and/or coordinating all research, evidence-generation, consultation, analytical and drafting functions required for the development and finalization of the CGEP. The Consultant shall ensure that all activities identified in the approved Inception Report are completed with the required methodological and quality standards and within the agreed timeline.

The Consultant’s responsibilities shall include, but not be limited to:

  1. undertaking a comprehensive literature and desk review of existing gender data, research, policy documents, analytical reports, evaluations and other relevant sources at national and sub-national levels, including the UNCT Gender Strategy for Pakistan 2025–2027, and reflecting its relevant priorities and commitments in the analysis and recommendations;
  2. identifying, assessing, validating and analyzing relevant quantitative and qualitative data sources, including official statistics, administrative data, household and specialized surveys, Census data and relevant UN and other authoritative sources;
  3. developing and administering KII, FGD and multi-stakeholder consultation tools in accordance with the approved methodology;
  4. planning, conducting, coordinating and documenting national and provincial consultations, KIIs, FGDs and stakeholder engagements across Federal/ICT, Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan, as agreed in the approved Inception Report;
  5. ensuring meaningful and appropriately inclusive engagement with relevant government institutions, civil society, women’s rights organizations, OPDs, academia, development partners, UN entities and other relevant stakeholders;
  6. translating relevant consultation inputs, notes, transcripts and substantive stakeholder contributions from Urdu and relevant regional languages into English;
  7. conducting qualitative and quantitative analysis of primary and secondary data and consultation findings;
  8. drafting and/or overseeing the drafting of assigned sections of the CGEP and ensuring analytical coherence across all sections;
  9. preparing consultation materials, presentations, briefing materials, documentation of meetings and consultation findings, and maintaining a record of substantive feedback received;
  10. undertaking fact-checking, editing and internal quality checks; supporting the independent peer-review process coordinated by UN Women and the GTG co-chairs; and addressing review comments to ensure the accuracy, consistency and traceability of evidence presented in the CGEP;
  11. incorporating feedback from the CGEP Task Team, GTG, PMT, UNCT, government and external stakeholders into successive drafts and the final validated CGEP.

Where the Consultant proposes to engage or utilize research, data, translation, analytical or other technical support for any component of the assignment, such support shall be managed and quality-assured by the Consultant and shall not relieve the Consultant of any responsibility or liability for the quality, accuracy, completeness, confidentiality, timeliness or delivery of the outputs and obligations under this contract. All deliverables and substantive outputs shall remain the responsibility of the Consultant and shall be submitted by the Consultant to UN Women.

Research ethics, informed consent and data protection: The Consultant shall conduct all KIIs, FGDs and other consultations in accordance with UN Women policies on research ethics, data protection and safeguarding, applying the principles of do-no-harm, confidentiality and voluntary participation. In particular, the Consultant shall: (i) obtain voluntary, informed consent from every participant before any KII or FGD, explaining the purpose of the consultation, how the information will be used and stored, the limits of confidentiality, and the participant’s right to decline to answer or withdraw at any time; (ii) protect the privacy, safety and anonymity of participants, with particular care for women and girls, survivors of violence, persons with disabilities and other marginalized groups, and avoid collecting personally identifiable information unless it is essential; (iii) store, transfer and dispose of all data, including recordings, transcripts and notes, securely, and share them with UN Women only in anonymized form unless explicit consent for attribution has been given; (iv) include the consent procedures, ethical safeguards and a data-management and data-protection plan in the Inception Report for UN Women approval; and (v) report immediately to UN Women any ethical, protection or safeguarding concern that arises during the consultations.

  1. Deliverable 1: Undertake a review of key resources (analytical reports, research, reviews, evaluations, and available statistical data sources) and prepare an Inception Report and an annotated outline, with the proposed methodology, and mapping of key stakeholders and institutions proposed for engagement (collaboration and/or consultation). The Consultant shall also develop the proposed KII, FGD and multi-stakeholder consultation approach and tools, identify national and provincial consultation requirements, and establish the process for documentation, translation, analysis and quality assurance of consultation inputs. Based on the CGEP guidance, the methodology would clarify:
  • How the CGEP guidance principles, international norms and standards, national ownership and capacities, and enhanced UN coherence, together with human rights-based and intersectional approaches, will be applied throughout the process, analysis and recommendations.
  • A proposed framework for conducting a gender-power analysis.
  • The Inception Report will also include a proposed roadmap for the process, with timelines.
  • The Inception Report and annotated outline will be reviewed by the UN, and feedback will be provided to the CGEP Consultant for incorporation before approval and commencement of the subsequent activities.
  1. Deliverables 2: Evidence Base, Consultations and Gender Analysis

Based on the approved Inception Report and annotated outline, the Consultant shall undertake and/or coordinate the required literature and desk review, data collection, stakeholder consultations, KIIs, FGDs and multi-stakeholder consultations at national and provincial levels. The Consultant shall document and analyze the findings from these engagements and integrate relevant primary evidence with secondary data and literature throughout the CGEP.

  • Analysis:
  • Country Context: Conduct an in-depth analysis of the socio-economic, political (including electoral systems), governance, financial, environmental and humanitarian context, using a gender lens, to identify opportunities, challenges and systemic barriers to advancing gender equality and the empowerment of diverse women and girls. This analysis will be informed by available, up to date national and sub-national quantitative and qualitative statistics sourced from official statistical databases, administrative data, and data from household and specialized surveys and the most recent Census gender disaggregated statistical data. Data will also be drawn from relevant UN Women global resources containing country statistics, e.g. the Women Count Data Hub. Gaps in gender data as related to the tracking of progress on the SDGs will also be highlighted.
  • Normative, policy, legal and institutional framework: Conduct an analysis of the alignment between the government’s normative commitments, and the national policy, legal and institutional frameworks, taking into account the outcomes of Human Rights Treaty Body reporting and other processes including special procedures. Additionally, reference will be made to monitoring and review processes including the outcomes of the annual sessions of the Commission on the Status of Women (CSW). The analysis will cover: (i) the national machinery responsible for gender equality, including its mandate, functions, level of authority, structure, capacities, resources, influencing capacity and coordination with key actors and institutions within and outside government; (ii) policy, legislative and institutional frameworks supporting gender-responsive budgeting, in line with SDG 5.c.1; and (iii) the national statistical infrastructure for gender statistics, including capacities for producing, analysing and using data disaggregated by sex, age, disability, race and ethnicity, where available.
  • An analysis of gender-power dynamics, and related structures and mechanisms that either support or prevent women’s and girls’ full and equal participation in all spheres of life. Analysis of gender-specific drivers of conflict and/or instability, key actors and factors contributing to inequalities and conflict, or opportunities for advancing peace, shall also be undertaken.
  • Partnerships for gender equality: Map key stakeholders according to their thematic focus, roles and strategies for advancing gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls in all their diversity, taking into account both traditional and non-traditional partners.
  • Civil society engagement: An analysis of the environment in which Women's Rights Organizations, Organizations of Persons with Disabilities (OPDs), and other civil society organizations working on gender equality operate, including a mapping of organizations by their areas of expertise, efforts, and funding opportunities. The Consultant shall undertake qualitative and quantitative analysis of findings from KIIs, FGDs, multi-stakeholder consultations and secondary data sources, ensuring triangulation of primary and secondary evidence where appropriate. Consultation findings shall be systematically documented and synthesised and shall inform the key findings, identification of drivers and recommendations of the CGEP.
  • Key findings: Develop draft key findings from the above analyses, highlighting interlinkages and synergies across the analytical components, the drivers of gender inequalities in each area, and key priorities. Identify opportunities to address these inequalities, including potential synergies, innovation and collaboration with traditional and non-traditional partners.
  • Recommendations - Develop draft recommendations for consideration:
  • • Identify national and other relevant policy, legislative and institutional reforms, together with measures to address social norms and implementation challenges affecting the identified gender equality priorities.
  • • Identify risks arising if gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls are not adequately addressed, to inform the UN Country Analysis multidimensional risk analysis.
  • • Identify strategies to strengthen women’s rights organizations and other civil society organizations and support their full and meaningful participation in national development efforts, including through improved financial and technical resources and stronger UN–civil society coordination..

The recommendations shall be grounded in evidence generated through the desk review, quantitative and qualitative analysis, national and sub-national consultations and stakeholder engagement. They shall identify practical opportunities for action by government, civil society, UN entities and other relevant actors, taking into consideration the global GEAP, the UNCT Gender Strategy for Pakistan 2025–2027 and other relevant frameworks and tools.

  1. Deliverable 3: Full Draft CGEP and Draft Knowledge Products. The CGEP Consultant will prepare the full draft CGEP based on the approved evidence base, analytical framework and annotated outline, integrating findings from the desk review, quantitative and qualitative analysis, national and sub-national consultations, KIIs and FGDs. The draft will include evidence-based recommendations, alignment of findings and recommendations with relevant global GEAP priorities and the UNCT Gender Strategy for Pakistan 2025–2027, a draft statistical annex, proposed visualizations and a draft presentation. The CGEP Consultant will ensure that substantive claims are appropriately sourced, traceable and fact-checked, and will present the draft analysis, key findings, recommendations and proposed visualizations to the UNCT and GTG for review and feedback. Following incorporation of this feedback, the CGEP Consultant will present the revised findings to external stakeholders for review and validation and address the comments received in the next iteration of the CGEP.
  2. Deliverable 4: Final CGEP and Knowledge Products. The CGEP Consultant will finalize the CGEP, statistical annex, visualizations and presentation following review and validation. The CGEP Consultant will incorporate agreed feedback from the UNCT, GTG, CGEP Task Team, government and external stakeholders; complete final fact-checking, editing and consistency checks; and submit a publication-ready package for approval through the agreed quality assurance and clearance arrangements.

The Consultant shall be guided by the UN Editorial Manual: https://www.un.org/dgacm/en/content/editorial-manual in developing the CGEP. Additionally, per the contract with the Consultant the title rights copyrights and all other rights of whatsoever nature in any material produced under the provisions of this contract shall be vested exclusively in UN Women. Institutional Arrangement:

The consultancy will be managed by the GTG Secretariat (UN Women Pakistan) under the overall supervision of the UN Women Deputy Country Representative, who serves as GTG Co-Chair. Day-to-day management will be the responsibility of the GTG Coordination Officer at UN Women Pakistan.

The consultant will work closely with:

  • The CGEP Task Team (co-chaired by GTG Co-Chairs and the Ministry of Human Rights)
  • UN Women Pakistan and UNFPA (GTG Co-Chairs)
  • The Resident Coordinator's Office (RCO)
  • Provincial Programme Teams (PPTs) for provincial consultations
  • Relevant national and provincial government institutions, civil society organisations, women’s rights organisations, OPDs, academia, development partners and other stakeholders identified through the approved stakeholder mapping.

The Consultant will be responsible for coordinating and managing all research and consultation inputs required for the CGEP and for ensuring their quality, consistency and timely integration into the CGEP.

This is primarily Islamabad-based consultancy with travel to provinces required for consultations. Travel costs will be reimbursed as per UN Women Policies.
