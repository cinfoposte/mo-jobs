In Ukraine, UNDP has been working since 1993 with the Government and local authorities, partners across the UN system, civil society and local communities to help identify and develop targeted solutions to the country’s development challenges. We work in close collaboration with several key ministries, departments, and agencies in a robust, integrated manner in three thematic priorities/portfolios:

• Inclusive Development, Recovery and Peacebuilding,
• Democratic Governance, and
• Energy and Environment.

The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in Ukraine, through its Energy and Environment Portfolio, supports national and local authorities in restoring, modernizing and transforming Ukraine’s energy and public infrastructure in line with the principles of sustainability, resilience, energy security and transparency. Since the full-scale war, UNDP has placed particular emphasis on ensuring continuity of essential public services while supporting Ukraine’s green and resilient recovery. Within this framework, UNDP works closely with the Ministry of Energy of Ukraine, the Ministry for Development of Communities and Territories of Ukraine (MDCT), local authorities and other national and international partners to implement programmes aimed at improving the energy performance of public infrastructure, increasing the use of renewable energy sources and strengthening the capacity of municipalities to plan and manage sustainable energy investments.
The Project Manager will lead the implementation of the Accelerating the Energy Decentralization and Green Transformation in Ukraine project, that supports the development and deployment of decentralized renewable energy solutions as part of Ukraine’s green recovery and energy transformation. The project will promote localized renewable energy solutions in selected oblasts and municipalities, with interventions identified through participatory approaches and tailored to local energy needs and conditions. The project combines infrastructure investments with technical assistance and institutional capacity development. It will assess the technical and economic feasibility of renewable energy solutions and develop recommendations on de-risking mechanisms, financing approaches and policy or regulatory measures required to create a more enabling environment for renewable energy investment at the sub-national level.

Job Purpose

The Project Manager will directly manage the Accelerating the Energy Decentralization and Green Transformation in Ukraine project and other related projects within the Energy and Environment Portfolio, in line with evolving projects needs and the portfolio’s approach and contribute to the efforts of the UNDP Country Office in the respective programmatic area. The Project Manager will supervise the project team, coordinate implementation with participating UN agencies and relevant partners, and support successful project delivery through strategic planning, implementation oversight, capacity development, monitoring and assurance, advocacy, and knowledge management.

The incumbent will work under the overall guidance and direct supervision of the UNDP Deputy Resident Representative (Programme) and in close day-to-day collaboration with the Energy and Environment Portfolio Specialist (Team Leader) in the UNDP country office.

The Project Manager will prioritize gender equality and women’s empowerment within all implementations and promote UNDP’s mandate as a gender-responsive organization with zero tolerance for any kind of discriminatory and abusive behavior, including sexual harassment, sexual exploitation, and abuse.

Impact of results:
Effective performance of the Project Manager will contribute to the timely and high-quality delivery of project results, strengthened partnerships with national and international stakeholders, improved institutional and technical capacities, and the advancement of Ukraine’s decentralized, resilient and green energy transformation.

UNDP adopts a portfolio approach to accommodate changing business needs and leverage linkages across interventions to achieve its strategic goals. Therefore, UNDP personnel are expected to work across units, functions, teams, and projects in multidisciplinary teams to strengthen horizontal collaboration and maximize synergies across the portfolio.

  • Support outreach activities, including relevant seminars, workshops, conferences and public-facing events, and contribute to preparation of communication and advocacy materials.
