Background:

UN Women, grounded in the vision of equality enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations, works for the elimination of discrimination against women and girls; the empowerment of women; and the achievement of equality between women and men as partners and beneficiaries of development, human rights, humanitarian action and peace and security.

In Ukraine, by 2029, UN Women aims to ensure that more women and girls, including those at the greatest risk of being left behind, lead, influence and benefit from inclusive, gender-responsive humanitarian action, governance, recovery, rights and economic growth.

As part of its work in Ukraine, UN Women supports national and local stakeholders in strengthening institutional capacities to advance gender equality and women’s empowerment, integrate gender perspectives into policies, programmes and organizational practices, and implement Ukraine’s national and international commitments on equal rights and opportunities for women and men. This work involves cooperation with public authorities, local self-government bodies, civil society, enterprises, institutions and organizations across different sectors.

UN Women has supported governmental institutions in the development and updating of the State Strategy on Ensuring Equal Rights and Opportunities for Women and Men until 2030 and its respective Action Plans. The Strategy provides a comprehensive framework for coordinated actions by central executive authorities, local authorities, civil society organizations and other stakeholders to advance gender equality in Ukrainian society.

In accordance with Activity 4, Task 3 (Strategic and Operational Objective 1) of the Operational Plan for the implementation of the State Strategy on Ensuring Equal Rights and Opportunities for Women and Men for the period up to 2030 for 2025–2027, gender audits are envisaged as one of the instruments for strengthening institutional capacity to integrate the principles of equal rights and opportunities for women and men into organizational policies and practices.

A gender audit assesses how an enterprise, institution or organization integrates gender equality principles into its internal policies, procedures, organizational culture, human resources practices, working environment, decision-making processes and other relevant areas of operation. By identifying gaps and good practices, it provides a basis for practical recommendations and measures to strengthen gender mainstreaming and ensure equal rights and opportunities for women and men.

Gender audits may therefore be relevant to a broad range of enterprises, institutions and organizations, regardless of their sector, size or form of ownership, that seek to strengthen gender-responsive institutional practices and create more equal, inclusive and non-discriminatory working environments.

UN Women intends to provide technical support for gender audits in selected enterprises, institutions and organizations, regardless of their form of ownership, to support national gender equality commitments and respond to requests from partner organizations and institutions. Organizations receiving support will be identified on the basis of relevant requests, programme priorities and the agreed scope of engagement.

For this purpose, UN Women is seeking Gender Audit Consultant(s), engaged as national consultants under retainer arrangements, to support the preparation and conduct of gender audits and the development of practical recommendations and action plans for enterprises, institutions and organizations in Ukraine.

The gender audits will be carried out on the basis of two resources: the Methodological Recommendations for Conducting Gender Audits by Enterprises, Institutions, and Organizations, approved by Order of the Ministry of Social Policy of Ukraine No. 448 of 9 August 2021 (the Methodological Recommendations)and the Step-by-Step Guide “Gender Audit: Roadmap and Practical Advice”, developed by UN Women in cooperation with the Office of the Government Commissioner for Gender Equality Policy and the Ministry of Social Policy, Family and Unity of Ukraine, with financial support from Sweden.

The consultant will report to the Programme Specialist and will be supported by the designated Programme Analyst, who will serve as the focal point for coordination, contractual and payment-related matters for each assignment.

For each assignment, UN Women will specify whether the consultant will act as the solo gender audit practitioner or as a member of a gender audit team working with another national expert. The written assignment brief will identify the consultant’s individual responsibilities, outputs and coordination arrangements. Within the agreed role, the consultant will provide technical and methodological support to selected enterprises, institutions and organizations, regardless of their form of ownership, throughout the gender audit process, in accordance with the applicable methodology and the scope agreed with UN Women and the respective organization.

Description of Responsibilities /Scope of Work

Depending on the agreed scope and assigned role, the consultant will contribute to the following tasks in coordination with UN Women and the participating organization:

  • Support the preparation of an organization-specific gender audit methodology and workplan, adapting the Methodological Recommendations and Step-by-Step Guide identified above, including a list of documents and materials for desk review, a survey questionnaire, interview guides and other required tools.
  • Support a validation session with the participating organization’s gender audit working group and incorporate agreed comments into the draft methodology for technical review based on the comments received.
  • Provide technical inputs during expert discussions, consultations, and working group meetings throughout the audit process.
  • Support facilitation of the knowledge-sharing activities, gender audit training sessions and other related events for the participating organization’s working group and other stakeholders.
  • Support the desk review of organizational and regulatory documents, materials and data in accordance with the areas of analysis specified in the agreed methodology.
  • Support the administration of the online survey and conduct key informant interviews and, where needed, focus group discussions and workshops, in accordance with the guidance provided in the agreed methodology
  • Support the analyses of the collected primary data and support triangulation of findings from the desk review, survey, individual interviews and other data-collection methods.
  • Assist with drafting the gender audit report drawing on the analysis of internal documents, administrative statistics, and collected primary data, including good practices, areas for development and recommendations.
  • Support the validation of the draft report with the participating organization and assist with finalizing the report based on the comments received.
  • Support the organization of the presentation of gender audit’s key findings and recommendations;
  • Support the development and finalization of an action plan to address the issues identified through the audit and implement its recommendations;
  • Provide agreed follow-up support to implement gender audit recommendations, including developing or updating policy documents and strategies, delivering training and facilitating consultations, where needed and agreed with the participating organization.

This ceiling does not constitute a guaranteed allocation of working days. UN Women does not guarantee any minimum quantity or value of services. Engagement under this retainer does not constitute a continuous full-time assignment.
    Before each assignment begins, UN Women and the consultant will agree on a written assignment brief specifying the organization receiving support, the consultant’s role, tasks, expected outputs, acceptance criteria, delivery dates, authorized working days, and any approved travel. The brief will remain within the scope and ceilings of the signed contract and will not itself amend the contract. Work will begin only following UN Women’s written authorization and completion of any required contractual approvals. Changes to an assignment’s scope, working days or deadlines require prior written agreement and any necessary contractual approval. UN Women will maintain a cumulative record of days commissioned and used against each consultant’s approved contractual ceiling. Requests from participating organizations or public institutions do not independently authorize additional work or changes to the contract.
    Outputs
    Each written assignment brief will specify the outputs required from the consultant, their delivery dates and the authorized working days. Depending on the agreed scope, outputs may comprise one or more of the following:
  • An organization-specific audit workplan and adapted methodology;
  • Document review findings;
  • Survey and interview tools;
  • A documented analysis of collected data, either as a standalone analytical product or as an identifiable component of the gender audit report, as specified in the assignment brief;
  • A draft and/or final gender audit report;
  • A presentation of findings and documented responses to validation comments;
  • A draft institutional action plan and, where commissioned, a finalized action plan reflecting consolidated review comments;
  • Agreed training materials, policy revisions and follow-up advisory products.

Outputs will be reviewed against the scope, methodology, acceptance criteria and deadlines agreed in the assignment brief. The consultant will address consolidated review comments within the agreed scope and timetable. Validation by the participating organization does not replace UN Women’s acceptance of the consultant’s outputs for contractual and payment purposes.

Assignments will be remunerated at the daily rate specified in the signed contract. Payment will be based on the actual authorized working days satisfactorily performed, supported by accepted outputs and a record of days worked. The UN Women Programme Specialist will certify satisfactory completion in accordance with the contract and applicable UN Women procedures. Payments will remain within the authorized assignment amounts and the overall contractual financial ceiling.

The assignment brief will specify the reporting and payment schedule, including the submission dates for agreed outputs and records of days worked. For assignments spanning more than one reporting period, any interim payment will relate to authorized days satisfactorily performed and the corresponding outputs or progress evidence specified in the brief.

All reports submitted to UN Women for assignment reporting purposes will be in English and accompanied by the complete set of supporting documents relevant to the assignment, including the gender audit methodology, questionnaires, gender analysis results, meeting agendas and gender audit reports, as applicable. Supporting documents, including organization-specific gender audit reports, may be submitted in Ukrainian. The assignment brief will specify the language required for each output.

UN Women will provide the consultant with available background materials relevant to each authorized assignment, including the methodological resources referenced in these Terms of Reference.

For each authorized assignment, UN Women will facilitate initial contact with the participating organization and clarify arrangements for access to relevant documents, data and personnel. The consultant will coordinate data collection and consultations with the designated organizational focal point, subject to applicable confidentiality and access restrictions.
