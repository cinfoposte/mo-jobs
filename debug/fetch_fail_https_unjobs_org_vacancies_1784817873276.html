Background

UNDP is the knowledge frontier organization for sustainable development in the UN Development System and serves as the integrator for collective action to realize the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Within the full UNDP scope of UNDP's portfolio, the organization delivers integrated solutions to the interconnected environmental and social challenges of climate change, disasters, biodiversity loss, energy transition including access and efficiency, public health, food security, and chemicals and waste pollution - while advancing development priorities on governance, prosperity, and resilience.

UNDP supports countries to identify the right mix of policies, institutions, and financial frameworks to scale up climate and nature action, reducing chemical and waste pollution. UNDP helps translate global commitments under the Paris Agreement, the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework, the Montreal Protocol, Basel, Rotterdam, Stockholm Conventions and other international accords into effective local action, harnessing new ideas to tackle the financing challenge and supporting just and sustainable transitions that leave no one behind.

The Planet Hub is a key strategist, architect, enabler, and implementer that supports the achievement of the Strategic Plan 2026-2029 of UNDP. The Planet Hub drives UNDP's vision of a world where economies and ecosystems thrive together — where energy systems are clean and accessible, nature is protected and restored, communities are resilient to climate impacts, and pollution is minimized.

Cooling, Circularity & Pollution Prevention (C2P) is a key pillar in the Planet Hub which focuses on greening the supply chain of various industries and products to eliminate harmful chemicals, reducing plastic and waste pollution, and promoting sustainable cooling and circular economy. The team manages the portfolio of UNDP for the Multilateral Fund of the Montreal Protocol, providing policy and technical support on projects funded by the Global Environment Facility (GEF) in the Chemicals and waste focal area, and relevant bilateral projects [1].

UNDP's Sustainable Finance Hub (SFH) draws on a critical mass of UNDP expertise, initiatives, and partnerships to support the mobilization and leveraging of resources for the SDGs, Paris Agreement and Global Biodiversity Framework. It works through four service offers: public finance for the SDGs, private finance for the SDGs, integrated national financing frameworks and the SDG Finance Academy. SFH is an integral part of both the Bureau for Policy and Programme Support (BPPS) and the Bureau of External Relations and Advocacy (BERA), as well as part of the Global Policy Network (GPN). The Hub serves as a connector, broker and global coordinator among internal and external actors; curates and manages UNDP's catalogue of service offers on SDG financing to governments, investors and companies, via country offices, to significantly enhance the scale and impact of UNDP's work. UNDP's Sustainable Finance Hub (SFH) sits at the core of the GPN and brings together expertise, initiatives and partnerships that mobilize and align public and private resources for sustainable development, climate action, biodiversity conservation, and pollution elimination.

In December 2024, UNDP's global project Shifting to Zero Waste Against Pollution (SWAP) Initiative was approved by the GEF. SWAP aims to reduce chemical pollution in the value chain, and improve resource efficiency, by supporting pilot cities in emerging economies and Least Developed Countries towards a zero-waste vision in line with green chemistry, circular economy, and waste hierarchy principles for sustainable production and consumption. The comprehensive approach of the Initiative will be implemented in five cities: Freetown (Sierra Leone), Kocaeli (Türkiye), Montevideo (Uruguay), Tunis (Tunisia) and Tianjin (China). The global component of SWAP is implemented by the C2P pillar (Planet Hub) in cooperation with the SFH. In line with UNDP's broader integrated finance approach, SWAP will also seek, where relevant, to connect city-level financing needs with wider national financing and planning processes, including financing strategies, Integrated National Financing Frameworks (INFFs), Development Finance Assessments (DFAs), and Country Platforms, as appropriate. This is consistent with the Sevilla Platform for Action initiative on Integrated Finance for Climate, Nature and Development.

The SWAP Initiative will establish a Zero Waste Finance Platform to connect local public and private stakeholders with investors, Public Private Partnerships (PPPs), and fiscal/financial incentives for circular economy solutions. SFH plays a leading role in developing, testing, and scaling financial instruments and enabling frameworks under SWAP, in close collaboration with the Planet Hub. Where relevant, the Platform will also help connect municipal zero-waste investments with broader country-level financing frameworks and priorities, including those reflected in development, climate, and biodiversity strategies. The Project Analyst for the Shifting to Zero Waste Against Pollution (SWAP) project will support both the global SWAP project implementation and the Zero Waste Finance Platform.

Institutional Arrangements

The selected Project Analyst will work closely with the Policy Advisor, Environment and Development Finance and the SWAP global Project Coordinator to discuss and agree on a final detailed work plan, task requirements, working methodology and reporting lines. The Project Analyst will report directly to the SWAP global Project Coordinator with a secondary reporting line to the Policy Advisor, Environment and Development Finance, working in close collaboration with SFH and Planet Hub colleagues and Country Offices implementing SWAP. Finance-related tasks will be undertaken in coordination with relevant SFH and Planet Hub Finance Pillar colleagues to ensure coherence, where applicable, with wider country-level financing processes.

The SWAP global Project Coordinator shall monitor, in coordination with the Policy Advisor (SFH) every stage of implementation of the tasks and shall review the completion of activities and deliverables and approve the deliverables. Throughout the assignment, the selected PSA holder shall participate in the global monthly calls and maintain regular contacts with both the project national teams (a frequency of weekly calls is anticipated) and the SWAP global project management team (ad hoc calls depending on needs).

The Project Analyst shall highlight issues or potential problems she/he foresees that could impact on project progress and/or effectiveness.

The Project Analyst shall be working home-based and will liaise virtually with the SWAP global Project Coordinator as needed.
