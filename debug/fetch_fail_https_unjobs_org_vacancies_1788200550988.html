The International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD) is an international financial institution and a specialized United Nations agency dedicated to eradicating rural poverty and hunger. IFAD invests in rural people to promote inclusive, sustainable and resilient livelihoods by financing programmes and projects that increase agricultural productivity, improve food security, strengthen rural economies and contribute to sustainable rural transformation.

IFAD finances investment projects implemented by governments and development partners across Africa, Asia and the Pacific, Latin America and the Caribbean, and the Near East, North Africa and Europe. These investments cover a broad range of thematic areas, including agriculture, livestock, fisheries, irrigation, natural resource management, climate adaptation, biodiversity, rural infrastructure, food systems, rural finance, value chains, nutrition, youth employment, gender equality, monitoring and evaluation, and impact assessment.

Geospatial technologies have become an integral component of IFAD's operations, supporting project identification, investment planning, targeting of beneficiaries, climate and environmental analysis, implementation support, supervision, monitoring, evaluation, impact assessment, corporate reporting and knowledge generation.

The Geospatial team, within the Office of Development Effectiveness (ODE), provides corporate leadership on Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Earth Observation (EO), Remote Sensing, geospatial data science and spatial analytics. The team develops geospatial methodologies, corporate standards, digital tools and analytical products while promoting innovation and the use of geospatial information throughout IFAD.

Although this roster is managed by the Geospatial team, consultants may provide technical services to any IFAD department, division or programme according to organizational priorities and operational needs.

Post's Organizational Accountability:

IFAD is looking for highly qualified, experienced and motivated Geospatial Analysis and Earth Observation experts to work as individual consultants and provide specialized technical services across IFAD's global portfolio.

The Geospatial Analysis and Earth Observation Consultant Roster serves to connect qualified geospatial professionals interested in supporting IFAD consultancy assignments and IFAD divisions requiring specialized expertise in geospatial technologies.

Please note that submitting an application does not imply placement on the roster or an offer of consultancy services. The roster assists IFAD divisions in identifying qualified consultants according to operational requirements. Upon identification of consultants with the required expertise, the hiring division will initiate the consultant selection and contracting process. Consultants may support IFAD throughout the entire project cycle, including project identification, design, implementation support, supervision, monitoring, evaluation, impact assessment, knowledge generation and corporate reporting.
