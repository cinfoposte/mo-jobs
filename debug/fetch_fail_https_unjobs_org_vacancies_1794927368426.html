THE BANK:

Established in 1964, the African Development Bank is the premier pan-African development institution, promoting economic growth and social progress across the continent. There are 81 member states, including 54 in Africa (Regional Member Countries). The Bank's development agenda is delivering the financial and technical support for transformative projects that will significantly reduce poverty through inclusive and sustainable economic growth. In order to sharply focus the objectives of the Ten-Year Strategy (2023 - 2032) and ensure greater developmental impact, four Cardinal Points, all of which will accelerate our delivery for Africa, have been identified for scaling up, namely Unlocking capital at scale, Reforming Africa's financial architecture, from demographic boom to economic powerhouse, Building climate-resilient infrastructure and value addition. The Internship program is part of a wider Talent Management Framework which facilitates the Bank to deliver on the Ten-Year Strategy.

THE PRIVATE SECTOR CREDIT ENHANCEMENT FACILITY (PSF):

The PSF is a captive credit risk participation Facility that participates in the credit risks of the AfDB's Non-Sovereign Operations (NSOs) in countries eligible to borrow from the African Development Fund (ADF) - namely low-income countries. Given its current capital base, the PSF's target portfolio size stands at UA 1.1 billion of credit exposures.

Given the specific nature of the PSF as a risk participant in the Bank's NSOs, the ADF Board of Directors exercises oversight over the Facility and approves each risk participation in compliance with the PSF Framework and Guidelines. The PSF Administrator (PSFA) is responsible for the day-to-day management of the PSF and for mobilizing additional resources to increase the Facility's risk-bearing capacity.

The PSF is financially and operationally autonomous, with resources fully separated from those of the AfDB and the ADF. It sources certain middle- and back-office functions from the AfDB. The PSFA works closely with relevant departments to ensure these functions are performed in a timely manner and in line with applicable standards, and that the Facility meets all performance, financial planning, reporting and audit requirements.

THE POSITION:

Under the supervision of the PSF Administrator, the Underwriter and Compliance Officer will be responsible for assessing the eligibility, credit risk and compliance of candidate transactions proposed for PSF risk participation. The role will support the full transaction cycle, including transaction screening, underwriting analysis, preparation of risk participation recommendations, review of legal and contractual documentation, monitoring of conditions precedent, portfolio follow-up, reporting, and contribution to policy, guideline and fundraising initiatives that strengthen the Facility's risk-bearing capacity.

The position is organized around six interrelated work areas: (i) transaction screening, underwriting and compliance assessment; (ii) preparation of risk participation documentation and support to contract execution; (iii) verification of conditions for guarantee effectiveness, payments and transaction consents; (iv) maintenance and revision of PSF statutes, policies, guidelines and legal agreements; (v) portfolio monitoring and reporting; and (vi) support to scaling up, fundraising and sub-participation arrangements for the PSF.

The Underwriter and Compliance Officer will apply sound professional judgment in identifying underwriting, compliance, documentation and portfolio issues requiring the attention of the PSF Administrator, and will proactively recommend improvements to the Facility's operational policies, guidelines, templates and management framework.

In performing this role, the Underwriter and Compliance Officer is expected to maintain a strategic institutional perspective focused on prudent underwriting, portfolio quality, operational effectiveness, development impact and alignment with the PSF's mandate to support the Bank's private sector, credit enhancement and resource mobilization objectives.

KEY FUNCTIONS: The Underwriter and Compliance Officer will be responsible for the following key functions:

  • Transaction screening, underwriting and compliance assessment
  • Identify, screen and review proposed transactions for PSF risk participation, with particular attention to eligibility, policy compliance, credit risk profile, risk-sharing structure and alignment with the PSF's mandate and guidelines;
  • Undertake due diligence on PSF participation applications, including the analysis of transaction documents, borrower and project risks, expected exposure, proposed risk-retention levels, guarantee coverage and compliance with applicable PSF policies and limits;
  • Risk participation documentation and contract execution
  • Under the guidance of the Chief Program Officer, prepare and review risk participation recommendation documents, including the underwriting rationale, eligibility assessment, proposed exposure amount, key risks, mitigants and, where relevant, preliminary draft Board resolutions for ADF Board consideration and approval;
  • Coordinate with legal counsel and relevant Bank departments to prepare, review and follow up risk participation agreements and related transaction documentation, ensuring consistency with approved terms and PSF requirements;
  • Conditions for guarantee effectiveness, payments and transaction consents
  • Review and monitor the fulfilment of conditions precedent and effectiveness requirements for approved PSF risk participations, including documentation, approvals, consents and other transaction-specific undertakings;
  • Assess requests for waivers, consents, amendments, restructurings or guarantee-related actions, and prepare clear recommendations to support timely decision-making by the PSF Administrator and relevant approving authorities;
  • Verify, in coordination with relevant Bank teams, that the terms and conditions triggering PSF guarantee payments are duly met before any payment recommendation or settlement action is processed;
  • Follow up with portfolio, financial management, legal and credit risk teams on guarantee calls, claims, recoveries, restructurings and related settlement matters affecting PSF-supported transactions.
  • Statutes, policies, guidelines and legal agreements
  • Contribute to, and where appropriate lead, the revision of PSF credit, underwriting and compliance guidelines, operational templates, checklists and user manuals for internal and external stakeholders;
  • Support the review and implementation of the Master Risk Participation Agreement and other legal or operational instruments governing the relationship between the AfDB and the PSF.
  • Portfolio monitoring and reporting
  • Provide timely underwriting, compliance and portfolio inputs in response to requests from internal and external stakeholders;
  • Contribute to the preparation of PSF annual reports, quarterly updates, monthly briefs and portfolio analyses, including information on approvals, exposures, portfolio performance, claims, restructurings and compliance matters;
  • Liaise with the Bank's portfolio, origination, credit risk, legal and other relevant teams, as well as external partners or counsel where appropriate, to ensure accurate and consistent transaction and portfolio reporting;
  • Support to PSF scaling up, fundraising and sub-participation arrangements
  • Participate in fundraising and investor outreach activities by providing underwriting, risk-sharing and transaction structuring inputs for potential scaling-up arrangements;
  • Support negotiations and contribute to contractual documentation associated with PSF scaling up, fundraising and sub-participation arrangements;
  • Undertake other assignments related to underwriting, portfolio monitoring, compliance, reporting and resource mobilization in furtherance of the PSF mandate.

  • Strong knowledge of credit insurance, risk participation, guarantee instruments, risk transfer and related business models, supported by hands-on transaction experience.
  • Proven ability to conduct underwriting analysis, due diligence, credit risk assessment, transaction structuring and compliance review.
  • At least four (4) years of experience in non-sovereign operations, investment appraisal, portfolio construction or portfolio management, preferably in development finance or frontier markets.
  • Practical experience in reinsurance, credit guarantees, loan syndication, risk transfer or comparable credit enhancement functions.
  • Knowledge of relevant sectors, including infrastructure, industry, agribusiness, financial sector development and private sector business development.
  • Strong credit analysis skills and familiarity with the execution, monitoring and settlement of risk transfer or guarantee arrangements.
  • Understanding of AfDB, or comparable MDB, policies and processes for non-sovereign operations, including origination, approval, financial close and supervision.
  • Sound understanding of private sector development in low-income countries and fragile states; transaction experience across multiple low-income African countries would be an advantage.
  • Excellent analytical, drafting and report-writing skills, with ability to prepare concise, decision-ready documents.
  • Strong planning, organization and stakeholder-management skills, including ability to manage concurrent workstreams in complex institutional environments.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications; knowledge of SAP or comparable enterprise systems would be an advantage.
  • Ability to communicate effectively (written and oral) in English is a requirement for this position. (Bilingual in English and French, desirable).
