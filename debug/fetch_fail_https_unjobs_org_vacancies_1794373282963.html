National Individual Consultant: Situational Analysis and Strengthening GBV Case Management Response Services for Married Girls in the Philippines, Home-Based (remote), Philippines
  • Organization: UNFPA - United Nations Population Fund

  • Office: UNFPA in Home-based
 

Purpose of Consultancy:

Background and Rationale

In 2025, the UNFPA Asia and the Pacific Regional Office (APRO) led a South Asia–focused initiative to strengthen gender-based violence (GBV) case management response services for married adolescent girls. The regional findings highlighted that existing GBV response services were rarely adapted to the unique risks faced by married girls—such as intimate partner violence (IPV), severe restrictions on mobility, forced pregnancy, and denial of resources or educational opportunities. Furthermore, the integration of survivor-centered GBV approaches with specialized child protection practices remained underdeveloped, leaving social workforces without tailored guidance or clear multi-sectoral referral mechanisms. Following the South Asia model, UNFPA APRO is expanding this regional effort across Southeast Asia (Cambodia, Lao PDR, and the Philippines) to ensure married girls are effectively included in national GBV response systems. In the Philippines, despite landmark legislation such as Republic Act No. 11596, which prohibits child marriage, girls who entered marriage or informal unions during adolescence—including adolescent mothers and girls living in humanitarian, displacement, or culturally distinct settings—may face complex barriers in accessing survivor-centered GBV case management and child protection services. To better understand these barriers and identify opportunities for strengthening response systems, UNFPA Philippines will engage a National Consultant to conduct a situational analysis and localized consultations across three diverse geographic contexts: the National Capital Region, Catbalogan City in Eastern Visayas, and Maguindanao del Sur in BARMM.

Objectives

The primary objective of this consultancy is to assess the existing GBV case management and child protection response systems for married adolescent girls in the Philippines and formulate evidence-based recommendations to strengthen localized response pathways.

Specifically, the consultancy aims to:

1. Completed Fieldwork and Data Matrix, including de-identified transcripts and/or detailed notes from 10–15 KIIs and 10–15 FGDs, coded using the shared regional analysis framework across all three sites.
2. Identify Systemic & Operational Gaps: Examine structural and social barriers, survivor-centered integration gaps, and workforce limitations in serving married girls.
3. Gather Multi-Stakeholder Insights: Conduct ethical and survivor-centered qualitative consultations with married adolescent girls, girls in informal unions, adolescent mothers, caregivers, husbands or male partners, mothers-in-law, community leaders, and relevant GBV, child protection, health, social welfare, and other service providers.
4. Formulate Strategic Recommendations: Provide country-level inputs and a synthesis report feeding into both national policy implementation and UNFPA APRO's regional case management guidance.
5. Identify and Strengthen Referral Pathways: Together with the international consultant, examine the accessibility, functionality, confidentiality, and survivor-centeredness of existing GBV and child protection referral pathways, including the coordination mechanisms between social welfare, health, police, justice, education, and community-based actors.

Scope of work:

Duties and Responsibilities of the National Consultant

Under the overall supervision of the UNFPA Philippines Gender/GBV Team Lead and in close technical alignment with the International Regional Consultant, the Consultant will undertake the following tasks:

Phase 1: Planning and Research Design

● Contribute to the development of a detailed sampling and recruitment strategy specifying participant eligibility criteria, the proposed number and distribution of KIIs and FGDs per site, participant recruitment mechanisms, and the rationale for the inclusion of each participant group, including married adolescent girls in informal unions and adolescent mothers.

● Participate in regional kick-off meetings and technical planning sessions with UNFPA APRO and the research team.

● Co-review, contextualize, and validate qualitative data collection instruments (FGD and KII guides) into local languages (Tagalog, Waray, and Maguindanaon context).

● Participate in specialized orientations on sensitive qualitative interviewing, survivor-centered ethics, child safeguarding, and Do No Harm principles.

● Map key informants, service providers, local government units (LGUs), and community structures across NCR, Catbalogan City, and Maguindanao del Sur. The final sampling strategy and participant distribution shall be reviewed and approved by UNFPA prior to the commencement of fieldwork.

● Finalize field logistics, participant selection criteria, and interview/FGD schedules in coordination with local partners and UNFPA field offices.

● Develop and finalize a comprehensive ethical and safeguarding protocol for the consultation process, covering informed consent, assent and caregiver consent requirements where applicable, confidentiality, privacy, safe recruitment and interview arrangements, and procedures for responding to disclosures of violence, imminent risk, or requests for assistance. The protocol shall include appropriate referral pathways and safeguarding measures for married adolescent girls, including those in informal unions and adolescent mothers, and shall be reviewed and approved by UNFPA prior to the commencement of fieldwork.

Phase 2: Data Collection and Fieldwork

● Travel to target sites and conduct 10–15 Key Informant Interviews (KIIs) with service providers (DSWD, CSWDO/MSWDO, WCPD Police, Health Officers, NGOs, BARMM MSSD).

● Facilitate 10–15 Focus Group Discussions (FGDs) with affected populations (young mothers, female caregivers, husbands/male partners, mothers-in-law, and community leaders).

● Ensure the systematic documentation of all KIIs and FGDs through de-identified transcripts and/or detailed field notes, including translation where necessary, data cleaning, coding, and identification of key themes using the agreed regional qualitative analysis framework. All data shall be securely stored and managed in accordance with UNFPA’s data protection, confidentiality, and safeguarding requirements.

● Conduct daily data cleaning, note-taking, transcript coding, and key theme identification using the shared regional qualitative synthesis matrix.

● Implement the approved ethical and safeguarding protocol throughout participant recruitment, informed consent and assent processes, interviews, and FGDs. Ensure that all consultations are conducted in safe, private, and appropriate settings, with due attention to confidentiality, voluntary participation, and the safety and well-being of participants. Any disclosures of violence, imminent risk, or requests for assistance shall be managed in accordance with established safeguarding procedures and referral pathways.

Phase 3: Analysis, Reporting, and Validation

● Participate in multi-country analytical debriefs and regional participatory analysis workshops with international research leads.

● Develop a concise 4-Page Executive Analytical Summary highlighting core country-level findings and policy recommendations.

● Review the comprehensive analysis report on GBV case management for married girls in the Philippines that will be drafted by the international consultant.

● Conduct local community validation sessions with stakeholders and affected groups to verify data accuracy and refine final recommendations.

● Develop key policy recommendations relevant to the Philippines context and a practical action plan to ensure recommendations can be adopted and rolled-out nationally.

● Ensure that all data analysis, reporting, and validation activities uphold confidentiality, privacy, and the protection of participants. No personally identifiable information or details that could identify individual survivors or participants shall be included in research outputs without appropriate consent and authorization.

Please note that if the consultant determines that additional experts or personnel are needed to complete all deliverables in this project, all associated expenses, including travel costs, professional fees, and any other related expenses, will be covered by the consultant.

Required expertise, qualifications, and competencies, including language requirements:

The following experience and qualifications are required for the IC:

Education: Master’s degree in Social Work, Development Studies, Gender/Women’s Studies, Public Health, Child Protection, or related Social Sciences.

Professional Experience:

● Minimum of 5 years of professional experience in GBV prevention/response, child protection, or adolescent sexual and reproductive health (ASRHR) within CSOs, INGOs, or UN agencies.

● Demonstrated knowledge and practical experience in ethical and survivor-centered qualitative research, including informed consent and assent processes, child safeguarding, confidentiality, safe engagement of survivors and vulnerable participants, data protection, and appropriate referral procedures for disclosures of violence or protection concerns.

● Experience in conducting research or assessments related to child marriage, adolescent pregnancy, intimate partner violence, violence against children, GBV case management, or the intersection of GBV and child protection is highly desirable. Demonstrated expertise in conducting qualitative research, facilitating FGDs, and conducting sensitive KIIs with vulnerable populations (adolescent girls, survivors of violence).

● Familiarity with Philippine national GBV frameworks (RA 9262, RA 11596, Magna Carta of Women) and local government mechanisms (VAWC Desks, LCPCs, CSWDO).

● Proven experience working in complex or diverse socio-cultural environments, including urban informal settlements (NCR), rural/coastal settings (Visayas), and BARMM/Muslim Mindanao contexts.
