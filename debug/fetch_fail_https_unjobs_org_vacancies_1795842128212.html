ABOUT WFP

The World Food Programme is the world’s largest humanitarian organization saving lives in emergencies and using food assistance to build a pathway to peace, stability and prosperity, for people recovering from conflict, disasters and the impact of extreme weather shocks.

At WFP, people are at the heart of everything we do and the vision of the future WFP workforce is one of diverse, committed, skilled, and high performing teams, selected on merit, operating in a healthy and inclusive work environment, living WFP's values (Integrity, Collaboration, Commitment, Humanity, and Inclusion) and working with partners to save and change the lives of those WFP serves.

To learn more about WFP, visit our website: https://www.wfp.org and follow us on social media to keep up with our latest news: YouTube , LinkedIn , Instagram , Facebook , Twitter , TikTok .

THE TECHNOLOGY DIVISION (TEC)

Technology is at the heart of WFP’s ability to directly deliver food assistance and services to over 115 million beneficiaries across 85+ countries. WFP seeks a leader who combines business transformation and technology leadership: a CIO and Director of the Technology Division (TEC) accountable for transforming how WFP delivers humanitarian assistance through digital technology and artificial intelligence, and for the reliability, security and operational excellence of WFP’s global technology infrastructure and services.

THE ROLE

As the Chief Information Officer and Director, Technology Division (CIO & Director), you will report to the Assistant Executive Director. You will own both the “why” and “what” of digital transformation – defining what needs to change in how WFP operates and driving adoption across the organization – and the “how” of reliable technical delivery, so that transformation is built on solid technical foundations.

Primary Accountability: Transform how WFP delivers humanitarian assistance using digital technology and AI, with measurable impact on operational efficiency, effectiveness and beneficiary outcomes, while delivering reliable, secure and cost-effective global technology operations that enable WFP staff to work seamlessly across geographies and emergency contexts, with zero tolerance for major security breaches or prolonged system outages affecting humanitarian operations.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES (not all-inclusive, within delegated authority):

Strategic Leadership & Vision

  • Define and drive WFP’s enterprise Digital and AI strategy, establishing WFP as the leading AI-enabled humanitarian organization.
  • Provide executive counsel to senior management and governing bodies on digital opportunities, risks and strategic choices.
  • Establish AI governance frameworks and support their effective functioning, promoting the ethical, transparent and accountable use of AI in humanitarian operations.

Business Transformation & Change Management

  • Lead execution of WFP’s Digital Business Transformation Plan, translating strategy into measurable operational improvements.
  • Drive digital transformation of core WFP operations, including:
    • DOTS (Digital Operations Transformation System) and operational platforms
    • Supply chain optimization and predictive logistics
    • Beneficiary identification, targeting and assistance delivery
    • Emergency response activation and crisis decision-making
  • Redesign business processes to be digital-first and AI-enabled, eliminating manual workflows and enabling data-driven decision-making.
  • Lead enterprise-wide change management to drive adoption of new digital ways of working across headquarters, regional bureaus and country offices.
  • Build digital and AI literacy and capability across WFP’s workforce, and establish metrics demonstrating measurable value from digital transformation investments.

Global IT Operations & Service Delivery

  • Oversee end-to-end IT service delivery ensuring WFP’s business-critical systems are available, performant and reliable.
  • Manage global IT infrastructure including data centers, cloud services, networks, telecommunications and end-user computing.
  • Ensure a field-first operating model with robust connectivity and IT support reaching country offices and emergency operations.
  • Lead IT service management functions (service desk, incident, problem and change management) achieving high user satisfaction, and drive continuous improvement in efficiency, automation and service quality.

Cybersecurity, Risk, Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

  • Establish and maintain a comprehensive cybersecurity program protecting WFP systems, data and users from evolving threats, including threat detection, vulnerability management and incident response.
  • Ensure compliance with UN cybersecurity frameworks, data protection regulations and WFP policies; conduct regular risk assessments and security audits.
  • Design and maintain business continuity and disaster recovery capabilities ensuring WFP can continue operations during crises, with regular disaster recovery testing and tabletop exercises.
  • Serve as WFP’s primary liaison with UN system cybersecurity coordination mechanisms.

Enterprise Architecture, Platforms, Portfolio & Financial Management

  • Oversee enterprise architecture ensuring WFP’s technology landscape is integrated, scalable and aligned with business needs; manage core enterprise platforms (ERP, HR, finance, collaboration tools).
  • Drive platform integration and interoperability, reducing fragmentation and technical debt; establish and enforce technical standards, governance and architectural principles.
  • Manage the TEC budget (approximately $110M) ensuring disciplined financial stewardship and value for money; lead technology portfolio management, prioritizing investments based on risk, business value and operational necessity.
  • Monitor and optimize technology costs, ensure transparent reporting on technology spend, performance and value delivery, and manage TEC procurement in compliance with WFP and UN regulations.

Strategic Partnerships, Innovation & External Representation

  • Manage WFP’s portfolio of strategic technology partnerships (major platforms, AI providers, innovation partners) and vendor relationships for infrastructure, applications and services, ensuring value for money and contract compliance.
  • Engage with donors on the digital transformation narrative, securing funding for innovation and scaling successful pilots.
  • Represent WFP externally in digital and AI forums, attracting partnerships, talent and resources; collaborate with UN agencies and humanitarian partners to advance interoperability and shared digital approaches.
  • Scan the emerging technology landscape (AI/ML, automation, analytics), champion responsible experimentation and rapid prototyping, and establish innovation governance determining which experiments to scale and which to stop.

Leadership & Team Development

  • Lead, develop and inspire a globally distributed technology and digital transformation team (300+ headquarters staff and 1,100+ field personnel) combining business analysis, change management, AI/data science, program management and technology operations capabilities.
  • Foster a culture of innovation, operational excellence, accountability, customer service and continuous improvement; build technical capability and career development pathways.

QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE

Education: Advanced university degree in computer science, information technology, engineering, or a related technical ﬁeld. First university degree with additional qualifying experience and professional certiﬁcations may be accepted.

Experience: Minimum fifteen (15) years of progressively senior experience in digital transformation, business process redesign, IT operations or technology service delivery, including at least 5 years at director or senior management level and at least 5 years in international or multi-country contexts.

MORE ABOUT YOU:

  • Proven track record leading digital/AI transformation that delivered measurable business outcomes – not just implementing technology, but changing how organizations work and deliver value.
  • Significant experience in change management and adoption – proven ability to drive new behaviors and ways of working across large, distributed, diverse organizations.
  • Proven experience managing large-scale, complex IT operations in distributed or multi-country environments with 24/7 operational requirements, with a track record of high service availability, system reliability and user satisfaction.
  • Extensive experience in cybersecurity management including security operations, incident response, compliance and risk management in high-threat environments.
  • Strong background in enterprise architecture, systems integration and platform management for large organizations.
  • Demonstrated success managing substantial technology budgets ($50M+) with accountability for cost optimization and value delivery.
  • Experience managing strategic partnerships with major technology providers, including negotiating complex agreements and holding vendors accountable for value delivery.
  • Strong understanding of AI/ML capabilities and limitations, data analytics and digital platforms – sufficient to ask hard questions, make informed decisions and translate between technical and business domains.
  • Experience leading large, geographically dispersed technology teams including senior managers.
  • Experience working in or with humanitarian, development or public sector organizations navigating complex stakeholder environments and mission-driven cultures.

HIGHLY DESIRABLE:

  • Direct operational experience in humanitarian response, international development or adjacent mission-driven sectors; experience managing IT operations in challenging environments (developing countries, conflict zones, limited infrastructure).
  • Experience in the UN system or an international organization with matrix governance; prior D1 or equivalent leadership experience.
  • Professional certifications such as CISSP, CISM, ITIL, TOGAF or equivalent; experience with ITSM frameworks, agile methodologies, human-centered design and iterative delivery approaches.

Language: Fluency (level C) in English. Intermediate knowledge (level B) of a second official UN language: Arabic, Chinese, French, Russian, Spanish, and/or Portuguese (a WFP working language).

LEADERSHIP COMPETENCIES & INTERPERSONAL SKILLS

  • Transformational leadership – Inspires and mobilizes people around a compelling vision of change; builds coalitions and drives momentum even in the face of resistance or ambiguity.
  • Business acumen and strategic thinking – Understands how organizations create value; sees connections between technology capabilities and business outcomes; balances ambition with feasibility.
  • Change leadership and influence – Drives adoption of new ways of working through influence, not just authority; builds trust across diverse stakeholders.
  • Operational excellence mindset – Relentless focus on reliability, availability and performance; holds self and teams to high standards.
  • Risk awareness and judgment – Demonstrates maturity in assessing and managing technology and security risks; balances risk mitigation with operational necessity; communicates risks clearly to leadership.
  • Crisis management and composure – Performs effectively under pressure during incidents or emergencies; makes clear decisions with incomplete information.
  • Collaborative partnership and stakeholder management – Builds genuine partnerships across organizational boundaries and with business leaders, partners and vendors; seeks to enable others’ success.
  • Ethical leadership and judgment – Demonstrates sound judgment on responsible use of AI and data in humanitarian contexts; prioritizes beneficiary protection and dignity; operates with integrity and transparency.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

  • Within 18 months, the Director of Technology Operations will have demonstrably:
  • Established WFP as the leading AI-enabled organization
  • Achieved 99.9%+ uptime for business-critical systems with zero major security breaches
  • Implemented a comprehensive cybersecurity program meeting UN standards with a successful external audit
  • Reduced technology operating costs by at least 10% through optimization while maintaining service quality
  • Built robust business continuity capabilities demonstrated through successful disaster recovery testing
  • Established clear technical standards and governance reducing fragmentation and technical debt
