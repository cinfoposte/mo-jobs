Mission and objectives:

  • UNICEF is committed to realising the rights of all children to help them build a strong foundation and have the best chance of fulfilling their potential.
  • We believe that ensuring a child is happy and healthy begins before birth: from ensuring their mother has access to good health care to reaching adulthood as a healthy, empowered and informed young person of the next generation. This journey relies on every child having access to quality health care, good nutrition, education, and growing up in a safe environment free from violence.

Context:

  • UNICEF South Africa implements a broad portfolio of programmes that advance child rights, adolescent development, education, health, child protection and social inclusion. Successful implementation of these programmes requires effective programme coordination, stakeholder engagement, knowledge management, reporting and collaboration with government institutions, development partners, civil society organizations and other United Nations agencies.
  • In addition to supporting its programme portfolio, UNICEF support the United Nations Country Team coordination mechanism that promotes collaboration among UN agencies to advance youth development, strengthen youth participation, support joint programming and coordinate implementation of youth-related priorities under the United Nations Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF).
  • Hosted by UNICEF South Africa, this assignment provides an excellent opportunity for a young professional to gain practical experience in child rights and youth development programming while contributing to both UNICEF's programme portfolio and inter-agency coordination. Working under the supervision of the designated UNICEF Supervisor, the UN Volunteer will strengthen programme administration, stakeholder engagement, reporting, knowledge management and coordination of the Youth Theme Group.
  • The assignment provides exposure to programme management, youth development programming, inter-agency coordination, partnership development and implementation of the Cooperation Framework. Through structured mentoring, coaching and workplace learning, the UN Volunteer will strengthen practical competencies in programme administration, coordination, stakeholder engagement and project management.
