For every child, the right to Education!

How can you make a difference?

The Ministry of Education (MoE) is implementing the Partnership COMPACT under the Global Partnership for Education (GPE), focused on Eritrea's priority education reform. In accordance with GPE requirements, Eritrea is required to conduct a Partnership COMPACT Mid-Term Review (MTR). The MTR is a mandatory requirement and a prerequisite for accessing future GPE financing, including eligibility for GPE IV funding. Failure to complete the MTR will limit Eritrea's ability to access future GPE resources.

The MTR was initially planned for December 2025 but was postponed due to low levels of implementation and the need to generate sufficient evidence on reform progress. The review is now scheduled for October 2026. The MTR must be conducted in accordance with GPE Guidelines and Quality Assurance Framework requirements and culminate in the preparation of an MTR Memo that will inform GPE Board decisions regarding future funding allocations.

UNICEF, as Grant Agent, on behalf of the Ministry of Education, seeks to recruit a consultant to provide technical support for planning, coordination, facilitation, analysis and documentation of the Partnership COMPACT Mid-Term Review.

Key deliverables

The consultant will work under the supervision of the Chief of Education, UNICEF Eritrea, and in close collaboration with the Ministry of Education, Local Education Group (LEG), GPE Secretariat and development partners. The consultant will undertake the followings:

Phase 1: Planning and Country Self-Assessment

  • Develop an MTR workplan and timeline.
  • Support establishment and coordination of an MTR Task Team.
  • Coordinate data collection and evidence gathering related to COMPACT implementation.
  • Complete the GPE Mid-Term Review Preparatory Monitoring Questionnaire.
  • Review implementation progress against the priority reform, enabling factors, gender equality, inclusion and grant performance.
  • Liaise with the GPE Secretariat regarding MTR preparation and review of the Country Snapshot Report.

Phase 2: Inclusive Dialogue and Draft MTR Memo

  • Design and facilitate stakeholder consultation meetings and MTR workshops.
  • Facilitate inclusive dialogue among government ministries, development partners and the Local Education Group.
  • Analyze achievements, implementation challenges, bottlenecks, lessons learned and opportunities for accelerating reform implementation.
  • Support identification of future GPE financing priorities and potential funding triggers.
  • Facilitate agreement on priority actions required to accelerate implementation of the COMPACT.
  • Draft the Mid-Term Review Memo in line with GPE requirements and templates.

Phase 3: Finalization and Submission

  • Revise the draft MTR Memo based on stakeholder and GPE Secretariat feedback.
  • Support validation and endorsement processes through the Local Education Group.
  • Finalize the MTR Memo for submission to the GPE Secretariat.
  • Prepare presentations and briefing materials for senior government officials and partners.
