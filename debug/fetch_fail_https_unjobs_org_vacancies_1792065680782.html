Humanitarian Affairs Officer, Kathmandu
  • Organization: UNDP - United Nations Development Programme
  • Country: Nepal
  • City: Kathmandu
  • Office: UNDP Kathmandu
 

The duty station is Rasuwa, Nepal.

Background

The Humanitarian Affairs Officer, UNOCHA is established to support the humanitarian response to the floods affecting Rasuwa and surrounding disaster-affected areas of Nepal. The position is intended to reinforce UN field-level coordination during the immediate response period, working closely with government authorities, UN agencies, NGOs, humanitarian partners, and affected communities. Under the direct supervision of the RCO/OCHA Bidur Hub lead and the overall supervision of the Resident Coordinator’s Office Nepal and the Head of OCHA’s Regional Office for Asia and the Pacific (ROAP), the incumbent will work in close collaboration with the UN Resident Coordinator, Government and relief/recovery partners to support the formulation of strategies of engagement in support of the mandate of OCHA at both managerial and operational levels.

The Humanitarian Affairs Officer will provide on-site humanitarian coordination in flood-affected areas, with a particular focus in Rasuwa and Nuwakot. The incumbent will help ensure that operational agencies and local authorities have a common understanding of priority needs, response activities, constraints and gaps, and that field-level issues requiring attention are communicated promptly to relevant coordination and decision-making structures. The role is operational and field-facing and requires regular travel to affected communities and response locations. The incumbent will also be responsible for providing advice and support to the Resident/Humanitarian Coordinator and operational partners to consider the priorities of the most vulnerable communities in humanitarian and early recovery planning.

Duties and Responsibilities

1.) Ensures effective humanitarian coordination at field level in support of national authorities
•Provide a visible and sustained OCHA /RCO coordination presence in Rasuwa and other designated flood-affected areas.
•Facilitate and support field coordination meetings with local authorities, UN agencies, NGOs, Red Cross/Red Crescent actors and other operational partners.
•Promote complementarity among responders, identify duplication and gaps, and support practical follow-up on agreed coordination actions.
•Maintain regular liaison with affected communities and relevant local structures so priority concerns can inform coordination discussions.
•Support field partners to steer a collective approach to Accountability to Affected People (AAP). This means joint community consultations, harmonized messaging in local languages, and shared feedback and complaint mechanisms, whose findings inform response planning and are followed up with communities. Work with the PSEA Network and agency focal points so that these channels are safe and accessible for reporting sexual exploitation and abuse, and that survivor-centred referral pathways are in place and known to communities and responders.

2.) Monitoring, assessment and analysis of humanitarian needs
•Monitor humanitarian conditions, priority needs, access constraints, emerging risks and response coverage through regular field visits and engagement with partners.
•Support or participate in inter-agency rapid assessments and field missions as required.
•Consolidate field observations and partner inputs into concise situation updates, analysis and recommendations for OCHA and humanitarian leadership.
•Flag critical gaps, underserved locations and operational bottlenecks requiring coordinated action.

3.) Support to operational response and emergency preparedness
•Promote timely coordination around immediate life-saving priorities and evolving operational requirements following the floods.
•Support contingency and preparedness discussions where continuing rainfall, landslides, access disruption or other hazards may affect communities or response operations.
•Encourage partners to integrate relevant humanitarian policies and principles, protection considerations, accountability to affected people and inclusion in field-level response planning.

4.) Information sharing, reporting and coordination support
•Maintain regular information exchange between responders in affected areas and coordination structures at national and regional levels.
•Support partners to engage women, persons with disabilities, older persons and marginalized groups in response decisions, using safe and inclusive channels that also serve as entry points for PSEA reporting and referral.
•Track how community feedback is acted upon across partners, and flag recurring concerns, including SEA risks, to coordination forums and the PSEA Network so the response can adjust.
•Prepare meeting records, action points, field updates, briefing notes and inputs to humanitarian reporting products.
•Support mapping of operational presence, needs and response gaps in collaboration with information management colleagues and partners.
•Track agreed actions and follow up with responsible partners to support timely implementation

5) Partnership, advocacy and enabling humanitarian access
•Build constructive working relationships with local and district authorities, humanitarian organizations, civil society and community representatives.
•Identify operational or coordination issues that may impede assistance and elevate them through appropriate channels.
•Provide field evidence and analysis to support advocacy on priority humanitarian needs, equitable assistance and effective response coverage.

Work implies frequent interaction with the following:
Staff at all levels within RCO/OCHA Bidur Hub, UN RCO Nepal and Regional Offfice for Asia and the Pacific . Representatives of Nepal National Disaster Management Organizations, other UN agencies and other organizations that work on disaster risk managemet and related issues.
Results Expected:
Provides sound analytical inputs and advice that contributes to improving institutional capacity and articulates humanitarian response to the Nepal 2026 Flash Flood with a special focus in Rasuwa. Provides timely delivery of assigned tasks, maintains partnerships and interacts effectively with colleagues internally and externally.

Required Skills and Experience
