ABOUT PROGRAMME AND POLICY AT WFP

Pakistan faces a significant burden of maternal and child malnutrition. Stunting, wasting, anaemia and micronutrient deficiencies remain widespread, and are compounded by poverty, food insecurity, limited uptake of health services, inadequate maternal and young child feeding practices, and recurrent climate and economic shocks. The first 1,000 days, from conception to a child's second birthday, are therefore a critical window for preventing irreversible growth and development deficits.

In 2020, the Government of Pakistan, through the Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP), launched the Benazir Nashonuma Programme (BNP) with support from the World Food Programme (WFP), UNICEF and WHO. BNP is Pakistan's flagship nutrition-sensitive social protection programme and combines conditional cash transfers with specialised nutritious foods, maternal and child health services, social and behaviour change support, growth monitoring, immunisation promotion, and prevention and management of acute malnutrition for pregnant and breastfeeding women and children under two.

Since its launch, BNP has evolved from a pilot into a national delivery platform. By June 2026, the programme operated through more than 560 Facilitation Centres and had enrolled over 4.7 million pregnant women and children under two. Independent endline evidence confirms that the integrated BNP model can deliver measurable impact at scale, including substantial reductions in stunting, improved birth outcomes, and increased full immunisation coverage. These results support continuation of the combined cash, nutrition, health and counselling package, while programme learning highlights the need for earlier enrolment in pregnancy, stronger compliance and follow-up, improved coordination with provincial health systems, enhanced data interoperability, and sustained quality and accountability across the Facilitation Centre network.

The third phase of BNP, started in July 2026 for three years, is designed to consolidate these gains and expand services, including to the whole of Pakistan. The new phase places greater emphasis on data-driven and digitised service delivery, Facilitation Centre optimisation, supply-chain efficiency and traceability, coordinated beneficiary identification and case management, stronger national and provincial coordination, integration with health and social protection systems, climate-responsive operations, value for money, and a phased transition toward stronger government ownership and long-term sustainability.

As the programme evolves towards the next phase of implementation and scale, there is a need for dedicated project management leadership to strengthen governance, strategic oversight, performance management, risk management, stakeholder coordination, and delivery assurance across all BNP workstreams.

THE ROLE

The position is based in Islamabad and reports to Deputy Country Director.

Under the overall guidance of the Deputy Country Director and in close

collaboration with the Head of Programme, the incumbent will serve as the Project Manager for the Benazir Nashonuma Programme, providing end-to end management and oversight of programme governance, planning, implementation coordination, performance monitoring, risk management, business transformation initiatives, digital solutions, and stakeholder engagement.

The position will act as the central coordination and accountability function across Country Office units, Provincial Offices, Government of Pakistan, cooperating partners, UN agencies, and other stakeholders to ensure timely delivery of programme objectives and achievement of strategic outcomes.

This is a unique position that combines programme management, project governance, strategic coordination, business transformation, and performance oversight to ensure BNP remains effective, accountable, scalable, and aligned with national priorities and WFP strategic objectives.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES (not all-inclusive, within delegated authority):

1. Programme Governance and Strategic Oversight : Serve as the central project management lead for the Benazir Nashonuma Programme; Establish and maintain programme governance structures, accountability mechanisms, and decision-making forums; Advise senior management on programme performance, implementation progress, strategic risks, and opportunities; Develop and oversee programme implementation roadmaps, milestone tracking frameworks, and annual work plans; Ensure alignment between programme activities, country strategic objectives, and government priorities; Support strategic planning for the future evolution and scale-up of BNP and related social protection initiatives.

2. Stakeholder Coordination and Partnership Management: Lead coordination among WFP functional units, Provincial Offices, BISP Secretariat, government counterparts, UN agencies, and cooperating partners; Facilitate programme governance forums, steering committees, and coordination meetings; Develop and oversee stakeholder engagement plans and communication frameworks; Strengthen strategic relationships to support programme delivery and sustainability; Ensure effective information flow and collaboration across all levels of programme implementation

3. Performance Management and Delivery Assurance: Establish programme performance management systems and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs); Monitor implementation performance against plans, targets, and commitments; Lead programme performance reviews and identify corrective actions where required; Prepare executive reports,

dashboards, and briefing materials for senior management and stakeholders; Ensure programme activities remain focused on achieving intended results and impact.

4. Risk Management and Compliance: Establish and maintain a comprehensive programme risk register; Identify emerging strategic, operational, financial, and reputational risks; Develop mitigation measures and monitor implementation of agreed actions; Ensure

compliance with WFP policies, procedures, donor requirements, and partnership agreements; Support audit responses and implementation of audit recommendations.

5. Digital Solutions and Innovation: Lead the planning and implementation of the BNP digital transformation roadmap; Oversee initiatives related to beneficiary systems, digital monitoring tools, traceability solutions, and operational automation; Coordinate technical

teams and stakeholders involved in digital initiatives; Identify innovative approaches to improve programme efficiency, accountability, and service delivery.

6. Business Improvement and Change Management: Identify opportunities to strengthen programme governance, efficiency, and effectiveness; Lead business process reviews and continuous improvement initiatives; Drive change management efforts associated with BNP expansion, redesign, and future phases; Promote the adoption of best practices in programme and project management. Retrofit programme design based on evidence generated, feedback from beneficiaries, and recommendations from the Government.

7. Cooperating Partner and Operational Excellence: Oversee the development of performance management frameworks for cooperating partners; Strengthen accountability and monitoring systems for implementing partners; Review operational delivery models and recommend improvements to enhance programme performance; Support development of future implementation models for BNP 3.0 and beyond.

8. Supervise a team of 10-15 people, set priorities and performance expectations, clarify roles and accountabilities, promote collaboration, and support staff development and wellbeing.

9. Lead cross-functional coordination within WFP and with other UN agencies, and with government at federal and provincial levels.

10. Perform other related duties as required to support effective, accountable and sustainable delivery of the Benazir Nashonuma Programme.
